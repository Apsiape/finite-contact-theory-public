# Mathematical Core

Status: first public extraction draft.

This document records the mathematical spine currently suitable for public
drafting. It is not a complete proof text. It is the bridge between the theory
spine, the theorem bank, and the verification package.

## Primitive Posture

The public core starts below the classical nouns. It does not begin with a
world, spacetime, Hilbert space, probability, observer, or completed
continuum.

The working primitive is finite contact under one-use composition:

```text
U0  DIFFERENCE      there are differences
U1  COMPOSITION     differences stand in further differences
U2  EXHAUSTION      every composition consumes its occasion
U3  FINITE REACH    every staged difference is reached finitely
U4  MULTIPLICITY    multiplicity is correspondence-structure
U5  MEASURE         admissible serial measures push through the trace
                    quotient to witness counting
```

The public theory must not silently strengthen these axioms into a completed
ontology. A total world, completed continuum, Hilbert host, or observer-agent is
admitted only after a construction or declared overlay.

## Core Objects

The first release should use a small vocabulary and state its burden:

| word | public meaning | burden |
|---|---|---|
| contact | finite staged difference-relation | provide the finite construction |
| occasion | a one-use compositional event | show what is consumed |
| witness | finite fiber element surviving the quotient | count it or say why not |
| reception | gluing/co-reception condition | never personify it as a mind |
| aperture | bounded visible sector / quotient | state what it hides and exposes |
| carrier | finite algebraic grammar grown from contact | attach scope; not "all QM" |
| completion | admitted overlay or horizon limit | separate finite content from ideal object |

## The Reconstruction Spine

The current public spine is:

```text
finite one-use contact
  -> trace classes and witness fibers
  -> coherent counting measure
  -> one-receiver gluing
  -> rational Born weights as counting through gluing
  -> CHSH-family boundary / Pell fence
  -> native carrier grammar at binary-Bell scope
  -> finite tensor certificates binding a floor-grown carrier
```

This is a theorem stack only at the scopes stated in
`docs/theorem-bank.md`. It is not a claim that all physical theory has been
derived.

## Scope Fences

The most important public scope fences are:

- **Finite staged forks:** public counting theorems live here first.
- **Rational scope:** Born and CHSH ladder statements are finite-rung claims;
  real numbers enter through admitted completion.
- **Binary-Bell finite carrier scope:** the native lift applies to binary
  two-setting sites.
- **Detailed-balance sector:** positive carrier readout and temperature are
  currently identified at tested reversible/zero-visible-EP scope.
- **Aperture level:** energy, temperature, and gravity-facing statements live
  one level above the primitive floor.

## What The Math Side Buys

The math side prevents the project from reading as metaphysics alone. Its
public job is to show:

1. which structures are forced by finite presentation and gluing;
2. which known structures are recovered rather than assumed;
3. where familiar continuous objects are admitted completions;
4. which residuals are permanent by the theory's own lights;
5. which claims remain private because their verification artifacts or scopes
   are not yet public-clean.

## First-Release Boundary

For the first public release, the mathematical core should be stated as a
finite reconstruction program with a scoped theorem stack. It should not claim
to solve:

- general quantum theory;
- quantum gravity;
- 3+1 dimensions;
- the continuum limit of interacting QFT;
- nature-facing prediction beyond the registered shelf.

Those may appear as named horizons, not as completed results.
