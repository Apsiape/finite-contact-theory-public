# Publication Policy

This repository is a curated public release, not the working laboratory. Public
material should be readable, scoped, rights-clean, and honest about burden.

## Intake Rules

A claim may enter public docs only when it has:

- a status label from `docs/status-labels.md`;
- a stated scope;
- a short public wording;
- evidence class or proof location;
- controls, contrasts, or reason controls are not applicable;
- residual obligations or failure conditions;
- dependency notes, including held-material scope dependencies when present.

New claims start at the weakest accurate label. Claims with unresolved evidence
or wording may be tracked privately or held for later release, but should not be
published as settled.

## Claim Wording

Public claims should say what is earned, where it is earned, and what remains
unearned.

Use scoped verbs:

- "proves at this scope";
- "recovers under these assumptions";
- "is demonstrated in this model";
- "is a candidate";
- "remains open";
- "was withdrawn."

Avoid unscoped verbs such as "solves", "derives physics", "explains reality",
"final", "complete", or "universal" unless the document also states the exact
scope and burden paid.

## No-Hype Rule

Do not advertise a result by hiding its label. Public text should not imply
academic consensus, completed physics, or unrestricted generality where the
claim register says `MODEL-SCOPE`, `EXTENSION`, or `OPEN`.

The preferred framing is conservative:

```text
Finite contact is a reconstruction program with labeled claims, public
controls, correction records, and explicit open hinges.
```

## Held-Material Scope Policy

Claims emerging from that private work may be released only at the scope their audit
earned. The current public split is:

- binary-Bell native carrier grammar may be drafted as a scoped theorem
  candidate;
- finite tensor certificates may be cited as recovery binding a floor-grown
  carrier;
- general selector, q >= 3, cross-site interlocking, CHSH-weight, and
  unbanked-certificate claims remain held or frontier.

The hold is procedural. It prevents public wording that treats frontier items as
settled before their gate has been paid.

## Corrections

Demotions, failed probes, wording corrections, and withdrawals belong in the
public correction ledger once they affect public material. Quiet replacement is
reserved for typos and formatting errors that do not change meaning.

## Rights And DOI Cautions

Public visibility is not a license grant. Unless a file states otherwise,
rights remain reserved under `LICENSE.md`.

Before any DOI release:

- confirm that included text, figures, data, and scripts are rights-clean;
- remove private working drafts, third-party material without permission, and raw
  notes not intended for publication;
- ensure `CITATION.cff` matches the tagged version and DOI metadata;
- treat a DOI as a citable snapshot, not as peer review or proof of a stronger
  status label.
