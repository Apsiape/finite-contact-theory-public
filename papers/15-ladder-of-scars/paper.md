## 0. What this chapter claims, and what it does not

`H^4(A_4;F_2)`, the periodicity of the cover's cohomology, and the
torsor-obstruction reading of `H^1` and `H^2` are textbook material. **The
chapter's contribution is the organization and the witnesses**: a single
ladder in which every rung — from the arrow of time below `H^1` to the
pentagonator class in `H^4` and the cover's own scar above it — is read by
an explicit, gauge-invariant, machine-verified protocol on one fixed finite
group; plus one lemma we could not find stated anywhere (the arrow is a

pinning theorem that reorganizes the program's first-extension line.

The recovery components are wins, not concessions: they mean the ladder is
made of load-bearing standard material, assembled in an order the
literatures it draws on do not assemble it.

## 1. The ladder

The program's published stack contains, in separate chapters, four
obstruction facts:

- **Chapter 5:** finite becoming-webs admit no global "now" — local time
  patches need not glue (the Möbius web).
- **Chapter 13:** the retained ternary contact forces the `2T` double cover
  of the contact group `A_4`; the retention residue is the unique class of
  `H^2(A_4;C_2)`.
- **Chapter 14:** the tower above it — `H^3(A_4;C_2) = C_2 × C_2` with a
  gauge-invariant bracket witness, `H^4(A_4;C_2) = C_2` — and the relocated
  measurement obstruction: no equivariant selector from law to history
  exists.
- **Chapter 14, boundary:** the no-selector law itself.

This chapter verifies that these are **rungs of one ladder**:

```
arrow      act-monoid non-invertibility        (below cohomology)
H^1        the now-torsor                      (time's selector obstruction)
H^2 = u2   retention                           (the quantum residue; forces 2T)
H^3        associator scars                    (bracket witness + cycle witness)
H^4 = u2^2 the pentagonator scar               (quadruple witness, value 1)
```

Three exact facts make the ladder a theorem rather than a metaphor (shipped
script, Sections A–B):

**The top is the bottom squared.** The extension 2-cocycle `alpha` of `2T`
is built explicitly; `alpha(g,g) = 1` on every involution — the Chapter-13
retention residue in cocycle form. Then `alpha ∪ alpha` is verified to be a
4-cocycle and *not* a coboundary: it represents the generator of
`H^4(A_4;C_2)`. (That `H^4` is spanned by the degree-2 generator squared is
classical ring structure — Adem–Milgram; the shipped content is the
explicit witness chain from the residue to the reading protocol.) The
quadruple diagonal pairing `omega(g,g,g,g)` is gauge-invariant by the
normalized-cochain cancellation, and reads `alpha(g,g)^2 = 1` on every
involution: **the pentagonator scar is read, value 1, by a quadruple
involutive self-contact — and it is generated by the retention residue
itself.** The tower is powered by retention.

**Silent classes have composite witnesses.** One of the two `H^3` classes
is silent on every diagonal word. By `F_2` duality the script constructs an
explicit 25-word bar-complex cycle pairing 1 with the silent class and 0
with the loud class — a class-separating protocol. Reading deeper scars
requires composite cycles, not richer self-contacts.

**Time and actuality are adjacent rungs.** The Möbius 3-patch cover's
no-global-now is computed by the same `F_2` pairing machinery as every
other rung: a Čech `H^1` loop witness, invariant under all
re-trivializations, reading 1 exactly when no global section exists.
Side by side, the `2T` extension admits no multiplicative section
(0 of 4096, exhaustively). "No derivable now" and "no law-defined outcome"
are one pattern — a nontrivial torsor admits no equivariant section — at
`H^1` and `H^2` respectively. The pattern itself is classical nonabelian
cohomology (Giraud; standard in the gerbe and anomaly literatures); the
identification of the program's two selector obstructions as its adjacent
instances is the claim.

**The arrow sits below the whole tower.** The coagulation floor (Chapter
14's cell 00) carries a strict intrinsic arrow — its cell count decreases
on every consequential merge — while provably retaining nothing; the
reversible floor admits no strict monotone invariant at all (every act
returns). So the arrow of time = non-invertibility of the act monoid: a
capability strictly below every cohomological rung, available to floors
that can remember nothing. Chapter 5's "arrow without thermodynamics"
deepens to **arrow without memory**. This strict capability separation is
the one rung we could not find stated in any literature (§5).

## 2. The second storey: the cover's own scars

If retention repairs `A_4`'s debt by passing to `2T`, what does `2T` owe?
The script computes the cover's own spectrum exactly (Section C):

- `H^1(2T;C_2) = 0` and `H^2(2T;C_2) = 0`: **the cover is
  first-order-scar-free.** Retention is complete after one repair — there
  is no tower of doublings.
- `dim H^3(2T;C_2) = 1`: exactly one second-order scar survives, on a
  structure that provably cannot carry a first-order retention bit.
  **Coherence memory without event memory.** (The dimensions are classical
  — the binary tetrahedral group has period-4 cohomology because it acts
  freely on the 3-sphere; the program reads that classical periodicity as
  a statement about its receiver, whose geometry is exactly this
  quotient.)
- The witness is honest about a registered miss: the program registered
  the guess that the unique involution `z` reads its own bracket scar,
  `omega(z,z,z) = 1`. **The guess missed** — the class is silent at
  `(z,z,z)`, gauge-invariantly — and the real witness, found by the same
  duality method, is a 4-word composite cycle on `Q_8` (restriction is
  injective at odd index). The record of the record is intrinsically
  relational: no single self-contact reads it.

Debt propagates up the tower not as more of itself but as one higher-order
residue. The identity-debt dichotomy the program carries as a research
direction has here its smallest exact face.

## 3. Boundary pinning: where the registered bet actually sits

Chapter 10 registered the program's first divergence: the odd
identity-holonomy class (cyclic triple phase `Phi = pi`) at overlap
`r = 3/5`, where `Delta_3 = (1−2r)(1+r)^2 < 0` — outside the PSD Gram cone
of quantum mechanics. Chapter 10 also noted that current photonics sits at
the `r = 1/2` boundary. This chapter explains both facts structurally
(Section D of the script, exact rational arithmetic):

**The pinning theorem.** In the quaternionic 2-dimensional irrep of `2T`,
every order-6 lift satisfies the operator identity `g + g^{-1} = I`
exactly. Hence for *every* state, `Re⟨ψ, gψ⟩ = 1/2`; orbit triples
`{ψ, gψ, g²ψ}` have Bargmann invariant `B = −c³` and singular Gram
(2 dimensions); and the odd class `Phi = pi` occurs **exactly at
r = 1/2.** The invariant `B` is unchanged by unimodular character twists,
and every twisted irrep of `2T` is 2-dimensional, so the argument covers
every twisted sector: `det G = Delta_3(r) = (1−2r)(1+r)^2 = 0` forces
`r = 1/2`. The pinned configuration is the classical trine (Mercedes-Benz)
frame of quantum information — the program adds only the observation that
a `2T` twisted-sector orbit *cannot be anywhere else*.

**The floppy alternative.** A real (T-symmetric / π-flux grade) carrier
realizes `Phi = pi` on the whole range `r ∈ (0, 1/2]` — exact rational
witnesses at four anchors on a 9-site ring are shipped — with the same
hard cap `r ≤ 1/2` (a square-positivity floor). Same boundary, no pinning.
(A small rigidity: the minimal 3-dimensional real carrier admits *no*
rational witness at all — `x² + 3y² = 2` has no rational points.)

**Consequences.**

1. The `r = 1/2` saturation of current experiments is *structural*: any
   Hilbert carrier whose twisted sectors are 2-dimensional sits exactly on
   the PSD boundary. The registered bet `r = 3/5` is exactly **one contact
   beyond the twisted sector's reach** — the carrier search is not an
   engineering problem inside quantum mechanics.
2. **r-rigidity is a carrier-grade witness readable in counts**: detune
   the preparations; a pinned overlap (`r` locked at 1/2) certifies an
   `H^2`-grade (twisted-sector) carrier, a tunable overlap certifies an
   `H^1`-grade (real/flux) carrier.
3. At the pinned point the three-window Gram is grade-blind — both grades
   present the identical class (`r = 1/2`, `B = −1/8`, `det 0`). **The
   grade is read by the fourth contact**: the orbit closure gives
   `⟨ψ, g³ψ⟩ = −1` (twisted; the central scar) versus `+1` (real). One
   more contact per rung — the same relational law as the ladder's
   composite witnesses.
4. This supersedes Chapter 10's "natural home is anyonic/topological
   matter" remark: a commissioned adversarial review found that anyonic
   fusion holonomy is transport-layer data and does not supply indefinite
   Grams; the carrier requirement is a *preparation-side superselected*
   Z_2 grade. The registered protocol of Chapter 10 (FCT-61) gains the
   sharpened joint signature already noted in the register — the pure
   `P111` collapse to `7/1125` with `D_2` invariant at `41/75`, at a
   ~2328-triple budget for 5σ — and, from this chapter, the pinning
   sub-signature: within any Hilbert carrier the odd-class triple *must*
   show `r = 1/2`; observing the registered `(r = 3/5, Phi = pi)` vector
   is therefore outside every such carrier, not merely outside a model
   class.
