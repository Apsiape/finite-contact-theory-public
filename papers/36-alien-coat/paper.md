# Chapter 36 — The Alien Coat

*Finite Contact Theory, v0.24. The program's founding question — which
features of the reconstruction are inevitable and which are contingent —
finally gets its designed instrument: transport the whole quantum-arc suite
to a genuinely rival generative floor and measure what survives. Two frozen
bets died; both deaths are the findings.*

## The rival floor

The **coagulation floor**: worlds are the same simple graphs, but a move
*merges* the two ends of an edge — both parents are consumed, shared
neighbors are kept automatically, and each unshared neighbor is
independently kept or released (one bit per neighbor; the canonical witness
is keep-all; the coat weighs paths by i per release). The floor is
**mortal**: population strictly decreases, every trajectory terminates. The
central law — every move stages a fork and nothing selects — holds natively.

## 1. What died in transport (two laws revealed as genesis-contingent)

- **Rest.** The coagulation floor has no class-preserving move at all: rest
  is not forbidden, it is impossible. The genesis floor's preclusion-of-rest
  theorem needed a floor where staying is an option.
- **Indifference.** The labeled coagulation kernel does not preserve
  counting measure (column sums 1/24 vs 1/12 on the reachable sets): the
  microcanonical/symmetry law of Chapter 27 is a property of *that*
  dynamics, not of dynamics as such.

## 2. What traveled intact (the generic core)

On 979 event-record channels across three starts and depths 2–3, in exact
arithmetic: the coat construction, the amplitude ceiling |amp| ≤ P, the
additivity dichotomy (additivity-exact iff phase-pure), and the **mod-4
phase-purity law** — brightness iff deviation count constant mod 4 — all
hold verbatim. The law is generic; its *carriers* are each floor's own.

## 3. The two grammars (recovery, named honestly)

Genesis hidden-bit supports in the reading census are cosets of linear codes
— **parity** correlation. The alien floor's supports include
**constant-weight slices** — **cardinality** correlation: the class of a
merge history constrains *how many* neighbors were released, not *which*.
In the census the two grammars never mix within a channel.

This dichotomy is a floor-level recovery of known structure, and we name it
at full strength: constant-weight supports are the **Dicke-state family**;
their marginal behavior (the Pascal-style split under reading one bit) is
textbook; the classical Fourier theory of constant-weight sets is Delsarte's
Johnson-scheme duality (Eberlein polynomials), as the parity grammar's was
MacWilliams duality; and the parity-vs-cardinality contrast echoes the
stabilizer-vs-symmetric (Pauli vs Schur–Weyl) dichotomy of quantum
information. What we add is the floor-side derivation: *which* grammar a
dynamics speaks is set by what its records can constrain — and a census
statement (every faithful support here is affine, weight-union, or their
intersection) whose completeness beyond censused scope is open by name.

## 4. The cap is floor-contingent

Chapter 33's cap theorem — genesis aligned solo channels never exceed
correlation distance two — is a *prohibition of the genesis floor*, not of
generative floors as such: the alien census contains **six bright channels
realizing distance 4** at coherence exactly one (relational distinction 3),
minted at depth 2 by merge-event records with all-or-nothing edge
feasibility. What one floor's records forbid, another's grant. Mortality
buys fine correlation.

## 5. The reveal law (the GHZ-versus-W contrast, recovered from grammar)

Alien bright channels are **reveal-robust**: on all 459 bright-channel
single-bit reveals in the census, both branches stay bright with the squared
NSIT defect non-negative — cardinality correlation stores phase in the
*count*, and revealing *which* cannot touch *how many*. Genesis parity
channels are reveal-fragile (Chapter 34's blindness law). This is exactly
the known persistence contrast between GHZ-type and W/Dicke-type
entanglement — one measurement kills GHZ correlations while Dicke states
degrade gracefully (persistency of entanglement, Briegel–Raussendorf; Dür;
Stockton–Geremia–Doherty–Mabuchi) — **recovered here as a theorem about
record grammars**: parity is fragile and cardinality is robust because of
what each kind of constraint stores. A recovery, and by this program's
standing rule, a win.

## 6. The discriminator transports; darkness does not

The full NSIT census on the alien floor returns the *same* sign table as
Chapter 35 — (zero, never-positive, both-signs) — so the actuality
discriminator is coat-level, not floor-level. (The first, narrower census
read differently; the discrepancy was traced to a bright-only sampling
artifact and is kept in the correction record — the instrument was caught
within minutes.) Meanwhile **no dark channel exists on the alien floor
through depth 3**: exact preclusion (Sorkin's term) is genesis-contingent at
tested scope. The mechanism points at conservation — the genesis floor's
measure-uniform channels can cancel exactly; the mortal floor's
heterogeneous weights never balance — and Chapter 37 prices that
observation honestly.

## Scope and honesty

Exact and exhaustive at stated census scopes (three starts; depths 2–3;
event-record keying). The alien floor is one rival; genericity claims are
two-floor claims, said plainly. The Dicke/Johnson identifications and the
persistency contrast are recoveries with the classical citations above; the
floor-side derivation and the cross-floor cap contrast are the claims.
