# Press summary — Finite Contact Theory

**One-line:** A solo, non-physicist researcher grows tiny universes from
a single minimal rule, measures them like an experimental science, and
sorts which features of physical law are inevitable in any such universe
from which are peculiar to ours.

## What the project is

Finite Contact Theory starts from one spare rule — a finite thing
touches something larger than it can fully take in, and the contact
leaves a mark usable once — and grows small digital universes from it.
With no Hilbert space, no probability, and no spacetime assumed at the
start, it re-derives large parts of the standard machinery of quantum
mechanics from counting alone, registers two preregistered predictions
that diverge from what any Hilbert-space model allows, and maps, across
seven rival dynamics, which features of physics are forced and which are
purchased. Every result ships as a dependency-free script that runs in
minutes. It is developed by an outsider to physics over about seven
months, using an unusual setup in which several AI models work together
as a research team.

## Three stories a reporter could tell

1. **The method — an outsider's research setup.** The author is not a
   physicist; he works in AI and software. Over roughly seven months he
   built a setup where several AI models work together as a research
   team and aimed it at a single question. The work is pursued under a
   strict honesty regime — scoped claims, plain labels, everything
   runnable — precisely because an AI-assisted theory from outside the
   field is easy to doubt.

2. **The radical transparency.** The project publishes what it killed.
   A public [correction ledger](correction-ledger.md) records 24
   claims that were retired, demoted, or narrowed — including some of
   the authors' best-looking ideas. One proposed universal theorem was
   caught failing by a margin of −495 during pre-publication review;
   that counterexample now ships permanently as a regression test, so
   the error can never quietly return. A theory that discards its own
   claims when the evidence turns is the story here.

3. **The science.** Universes are built to order with their physics
   predicted in advance, then measured — and the predictions are frozen
   before any engine runs. Two of those predictions are registered,
   falsifiable bets that point outside standard quantum mechanics, each
   shipped with its protocol, its nulls, and the conditions that would
   kill it — labeled as bets, not discoveries.
