## 1. The hierarchy (containment, the one-way mirror, the telescope)

For nested observers O1 ⊂ O2 on the six-mark clique:

- **Containment.** The sub-observer's experience is exactly the
  restriction of the super's. The contained is fully known to the
  container.
- **The one-way mirror.** Knowledge across containment is exactly
  asymmetric: the sub is transparent upward; the super is opaque
  downward by precisely its extra participation (fiber sizes 18 vs 3
  on the model).
- **The chain telescopes.** Along a nested chain, inter-level
  ignorance decomposes exactly additively with zero slack
  (2.585 + 1.585 + 0 bits on the model).

**Classification (per the blind sweep):** the containment/restriction
identity is the compositionality of natural projection in
Mazurkiewicz trace theory, and the asymmetry is the finer-partition
lemma of interpreted systems — a clean **recovery** of the standard
epistemic-logic structure (Parikh–Ramanujam 1985; Fagin, Halpern,
Moses, Vardi 1995; Mazurkiewicz 1995) on a resource-consuming floor.
The telescoping identity is presented as the entropy chain rule
instantiated on trace fibers (Shannon; Cover–Thomas ch. 2), i.e.
bookkeeping, not a novel result. The recovery is the point: the
standard multi-agent epistemic laws *fall out of one-use contact
counting* with no epistemic axioms assumed.

## 2. Tuition (the transfer channel, teaching = measurement)

- **The transfer channel.** Boundary contacts are co-minted, so
  teaching is key-free: what crosses the boundary needs no
  interpretation convention. Per-contact capacity equals the boundary
  fork width.
- **Teaching = measurement.** The information-transfer computation
  across the boundary is identical to the measurement computation of
  Chapter 19 (co-minting plus null-inference); the two differ only in
  which side is called the instrument.
- **The price of tuition.** A learner's budget is the boundary mint;
  in every budget-exhausting history the sub-observer's entire life
  was lessons.

**Classification:** the movability of the system/instrument cut is
von Neumann's, and measurement-as-information-transfer is Zurek's;
quantified teaching budgets exist as teaching dimension
(Goldman–Kearns 1995). The sweep found no prior identity
*teaching ≡ measurement with capacity = boundary width*; that
unification is this section's claim, at model scope.

## 3. The shared eye and the redirection theorem

Overlapping observers (|O1| = |O2| = 3, overlap 0/1/2) on the
six-mark clique:

- **The shared eye.** The common sector is witnessed identically, in
  identical order, by both observers in every history — including
  exterior events the shared organ touches. A shared sense is an
  automatic consensus channel.
- **The redirection theorem** (a registered miss, scored and
  upgraded in the research record): increasing overlap does NOT grow
  the common sector — handshake events trade against shared-organ
  events nearly one-for-one. What overlap buys is agreement *about
  the world*: jointly witnessed exterior events are zero at overlap
  zero and positive after, while pooled coverage strictly shrinks.
  **Shared senses do not buy more agreement — they redirect agreement
  from facts about the pair to facts about the world, and coverage
  pays for it.**

**Classification:** automatic consensus through shared environmental
access is quantum Darwinism's consensus mechanism (Zurek 2009) —
recovery, and the contrast is instructive since our agreement is
forced by participation alone. The **redirection conservation** has
no located prior art (the sweep's strongest priority candidate in
this chapter).

## 4. Membership opacity and the price list

- **Membership opacity.** Which marks belong to which observer is
  not written on the floor: trace ensembles of relabeling-related
  designations are structurally identical. "You saw it too" is not
  derivable from any experience; mutual knowledge requires a
  received membership key — a third column-2 registration beside the
  semantic key and the scheme.
- **The price list** (the capstone). Six observer operations verified
  coherently on one model: RECORD costs the seed; MEASURE is
  co-minting (and always informs, silence included); TEACH is
  co-minting across a boundary; POOL is free; the MIRROR's advantage
  is exactly extra participation; SELF-KNOWLEDGE is free up to
  embedding. **Every way of knowing is either participation (paid in
  consumed marks) or convention (paid in received keys) — and the
  ledger closes over both.**

**Classification:** the impossibility of establishing mutual
knowledge from interaction alone is classical (Lewis 1969; Halpern–
Moses 1990 coordinated attack; Clark–Marshall 1981); our contribution
is a new exact proof route on the contact floor. A named incumbent is
distinguished: Krakauer et al.'s information-theoretic individuality
(2020) infers agent boundaries from predictive dynamics; on our floor
the trace data provably lacks that signal — the two results are
compatible and the difference is located in what the substrate
retains. The two-currency **exhaustiveness** of the price list is the
chapter's second priority candidate (nearest rhymes: Russell's
acquaintance/description distinction, 1911; Landauer 1991; Lewis
1969); it is a taxonomy claim, and the named falsifier is a third
currency — inference from received keys about unparticipated events —
which on this floor reduces to the key currency (keys are the only
non-participatory input), a reduction that is model-scope only.

## 5. Scope

All results are exact theorems at the stated finite model scopes.
Nothing about nature is claimed; "observer" means the Chapter-19
constitution. The recoveries (containment, telescoping, opacity,
consensus) are labeled as such with their classical sources cited;
the claims are confined to the redirection conservation, the
teaching≡measurement identity with its capacity formula, the
membership key's column-2 placement, and the priced exhaustiveness
of the six-operation list.
