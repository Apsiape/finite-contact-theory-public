# Finite Contact Theory

Status: extraction plan (not the release narrative).

This document is the extraction plan for the public spine of Finite Contact
Theory. It is not the release narrative — the paper
(`papers/finite-contact-theory-v0.1.md`) is. Its purpose is to define the order
in which the public account is extracted from the private research corpus.

## Public Framing

Finite Contact Theory is a finite reconstruction program. It asks what must be
true of any reality built from finite contact, one-use occasions, admissible
composition, and local reception.

The public theory should not be framed as a finished academic consensus or as a
replacement for all existing physics. Its first public form should be framed as:

> a finite, non-anthropic reconstruction program that recovers, relocates, or
> dissolves major structures normally treated as primitive.

## Extraction Principle

The public spine should contain only claims that pass all three tests:

1. **Generation:** the object or law has a finite generation rule.
2. **Transformation:** its behavior under admissible composition, aperture, or
   coarse-graining is stated.
3. **Failure:** the condition under which it fails, demotes, or becomes an
   admitted overlay is named.

No public claim should be admitted only because it is beautiful, explanatory,
or already familiar from physics.

## Proposed Public Order

1. **Finite Contact**
   Difference, contact, occasion, exhaustion, and finite reach.

2. **Reception**
   The receiver is not an agent. It is a gluing condition over locally staged
   fragments. This is the first major de-anthropizing move.

3. **Counting**
   The measure is not assumed. It is admitted only where finite multiplicities
   push through the trace or reception quotient.

4. **Probability**
   Probability is presented as the received, stream-erased form of finite
   counting plus the explicitly named typicality residual.

5. **Quantum**
   Quantum structure is introduced through one-receiver gluing, exclusivity,
   witness counts, and staged contexts. Held-material wording should split
   the scoped result from the open general program: at binary-Bell finite
   carrier scope, native carrier grammar and boundary certificates are
   admitted as public candidates; general selector, weight-selection,
   cross-site interlocking, and q >= 3 extensions remain open or held.

6. **Time, Arrow, and Thermodynamics**
   The arrow is not introduced as a background coordinate. It appears through
   one-use order, trace-class obstruction, and recovered thermodynamic
   structure.

7. **Spacetime, Energy, and Gravity**
   Spacetime and energy are treated as aperture-level structures. Temperature
   and level-2 energy appear where visible entropy production vanishes. Gravity
   remains a staged frontier, not a solved public theorem.

8. **Mathematics and the Continuum**
   Completed continua are treated as admitted overlays. The public account
   should state which finite and constructive content is recovered and which
   continuum ideals remain overlays.

9. **Open Hinges**
   The public release should end with named hinges, not vague promises. Each
   hinge should have an evidence class and a kill condition.

## Public/Private Split

The following topics may receive scoped public wording:

- native carrier grammar;
- witness-overlap complementarity;
- binary-Bell boundary language at finite carrier scope;
- Sliwa/CHSH certificates as imported recovery binding a floor-grown carrier.

The following topics should remain open or held:

- the general almost-quantum selector;
- quantum weight selection beyond CHSH-family/binary carrier scope;
- terminalized one-receiver carrier/interface mechanisms;
- cross-site interlocking;
- q >= 3 and more-outcome carriers;
- any claim that the floor derives all of quantum mechanics.

## Public Tone

The release should be bold but exact. It should prefer sentences of this form:

```text
At scope S, mechanism M forces/reconstructs/dissolves claim C, with residual R.
```

It should avoid sentences of this form:

```text
This explains all of physics.
```

The work is strongest when it lets the ledger carry the ambition.
