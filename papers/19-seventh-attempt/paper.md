the seventh attempt closes **by inversion** — instead of deriving the

what cannot be derived. Every registered miss along the way is scored

## 0. The question, and the method that made it tractable

Every prior result in this program is measured from outside: a
God's-eye basis reads the floor. But the theory prices measurement
(Chapters 16–17) — so it owes an account of a measurer *inside*. The
question: **can a floor host its own registrar — a subsystem whose
records of the floor are floor events — with the ledger balancing
globally? And what does physics look like from the registrar's seat?**

The method is the chapter's first result. Six prior attempts failed by
letting the recording subsystem "choose" what to record — an outside
selector, forbidden by the floor's own central law. The fix: **every
claim is a capability claim** — existence or nonexistence over
*registration schemes* (sets of histories with coverage and
legibility), never dynamics of a chosen policy. The scheme's own
status is then computed, not assumed.

Setting (all results exact at these scopes): finite one-use marks, a
static tolerance, pair-consuming contacts, runs to exhaustion; an
observer is a mark subset; its *experience* is the subsequence of
contacts involving its marks.

## 1. Movement A — the price (Sections: registrar, anti-regress)

For a causally **decoupled** register (no shared contacts with the
world):

- **Schemes exist** — the floor *can* host a registrar, with exactly
  matched coding on the minimal model. But:
- **There is no free scheme.** Keylessly, every register trace is
  consistent with every world-history and every interleaving: the
  register alone identifies nothing. The missing information is
  exactly the *scheme key* — the interpretation map. The keyless
  deficit is a **floor invariant**: no scheme improves it.
- **The anti-regress.** Adding register capacity strictly *increases*
  the keyless deficit (5.17 → 9.08 bits on the shipped models): a
  register's visible capacity is always below the placement entropy it
  adds. The more an inert floor writes about itself, the more there is
  to know. The record-of-record tower cannot even start without a key.
- **The seed.** The key suffices exactly — and is *underived*: the
  law's automorphisms permute schemes without fixed points, so no
  symmetry-respecting selection exists. In the three-column vocabulary
  of Chapter 17, the key is a **column-2 object: a constant of the
  hosted world** — underived, identification-enabling, paid once.

## 2. Movement B — the books (Sections: balance sheet, interpretation
## regress)

- **Zero slack.** Minted = banked + deficit, exactly and uniformly
  (216 = 6 × 36; 19,440 = 36 × 540): the ledger frame has no hole at
  self-reference.
- **The key outweighs the world.** The scheme space grows factorially
  against the floor's polynomial history space (33.6M schemes vs 216
  histories; 90! vs 162,000): the complete interpretation map is
  *unwritable* — by the entire floor, not merely by a register.
- **No equivariant convention at any level.** Token registers for the
  key face the same relabeling argument as the key, level after level;
  the only invariant assignments are constants, and a constant still
  requires *which* constant. **Every interpretation tower terminates
  in a paid, underived registration.**

> **Every self-describing world runs on injected capital — and the
> capital is a constant.** The floor can carry unlimited records and
> records-about-records, but the map from marks to meaning passes, at
> exactly one point, through a paid constant.

## 3. Movement C — the observer (Sections: inhabitant, bandwidth,
## coupled semantics, dial)

**From inside, given the seed:** the law is readable in O(1) (the
first decoded record identifies the rule-family member); but the
observer's own scheme is **transparent** — every alternative key
yields a self-consistent decoding of the same trace, so the inhabitant
cannot falsify its own interpretation from inside. Two kinds of
constants are thereby distinguished: world-parameters (readable from
every window, Chapter 17) and **semantic keys** (operative as the
lens, invisible as an object).

**The bandwidth theorem.** A registrar exists iff total capacity meets
total mint, and stays *current* iff its fork width matches the world's
at every step. Under a matched register the hosted now **breathes**:
consistent-history counts run 90 → 6 → 6 → 1 → 1 through a run —
narrowing at each banked record, holding while the world runs ahead.
**The hosted present's width at any instant equals the world's mint
since the last banked record.**

**Coupling changes the semantics.** When recording *consumes* world
marks (cross-tolerance), two registered bets failed, and both failures
are the results:

- **Null-result information.** On an exhaustion floor with a cross
  contact available, a trace showing *no* crossing still carries
  information (0.585 bits on the shipped model): silence informs. The
  interaction-free measurement phenomenon has an exact combinatorial
  face. (Zero-coupling control confirms the decoupled sections as the
  correct limit.)
- **Measurement mints.** The frozen trade-off ("information gained ≤
  mint destroyed") is *false*: the coupled floor has strictly more
  histories than the decoupled product (270 vs 216) — measurement adds
  new, co-minted branch structure — and keyless information reads
  exactly those co-minted forks. **Information is key-free exactly
  when the register co-minted it: you can read without a key precisely
  what you helped mint. First-person knowledge is co-minted
  distinction; the key is only ever needed for others' mint.**

The two semantics **partition exactly** (consumptive + conventional =
total, per trace), each priced in its own currency — participation vs
seed. Swept 0 → 4 cross edges, the **measurement dial** runs from the
pure-convention world to the co-minted world (mint 216 → 528; fully
key-free traces 0 → 60), with the sharp rider that abstention-heavy
histories keep their full key burden even at maximal coupling:
**a hosted observer's key burden is exactly its non-participation —
what it did not help mint, it must be told.**

## 4. Movement D — the community (Sections: self-consumption,
## witnesses)

Dropping the world/register partition entirely (an observer is any
mark subset, its experience its participation):

- **The participation resolution.** The keyless residual is monotone
  *decreasing* in observer size (6.49 → 0 bits): capacity heals
  exactly when it participates. The anti-regress stands as a theorem
  about *non-participants*; participation inverts it.
- **Privacy's unit is the unobserved contact.** The residual reaches
  zero already when the exterior has one mark — a lone mark cannot
  contact privately. Opacity requires an exterior *pair*.
- **Biography and proper time are free.** Every observer's complete
  causal biography, in its internal order, is key-free and exact; the
  fibers factorize so the only unknown is the exterior's private life
  and where the observer's own life sits among it. **No one is opaque
  to themselves; the unknown is always when-relative-to-others.**
- **The witness theorems.** For two disjoint observers: every event is
  shared, private-to-one, or exterior-private (the four-sector
  ledger); co-witnessed events appear in both experiences in the same
  relative order in *every* history — **agreement on co-minted facts
  is forced, not negotiated (no Rashomon)**; each observer determines
  the other exactly where they touched (mutual opacity, symmetric
  first-person privilege); and pooled experience determines everything
  except what neither touched (**the pooling theorem**).

## 5. What is proven, what is not

**Proven exactly** (shipped `verification/seventh_attempt.py`, 29/29,
stdlib): every statement above at its stated finite model scope,
including the exhaustive scheme-space counts, the monotonicity sweeps,
the factorization of every fiber, and the forced-agreement census over
all histories.

**Not claimed:** that physical observers are mark subsets (this is the
mathematics of the floor's own observer concept, in the same standing
as the atlas); that nature's semantic constants are located (a
structural account is offered); any probabilistic or thermodynamic
content (none appears); and any resolution of *which* seed a world
carries — the seed is received, by theorem. The scored registered
misses (three upgraded into results) are part of the record, not
blemishes on it: the worlds beat our bets, which is what registration
is for.

## 6. Prior art (blind-sweep record) and labels

A blind, repo-barred novelty sweep was commissioned for this release;
labels follow it. Per standing policy, novelty governs the public label
only — several results below are *stronger* for being recoveries, and
one identity is demoted to bookkeeping at the sweep's correct demand.

- **The impossibility core: KNOWN.** That an embedded or decoupled
  register cannot carry full world-information is occupied territory:
  Breuer (1995, the impossibility of accurate state self-measurement),
  Wolpert (2008, physical limits of inference — the closest single
  frame to this whole chapter), Dalla Chiara (1977), Svozil's
  intrinsic-observer limits. Cited; recomputed here as checks.
  **The price structure is the claim:** the deficit as an exact
  computable floor invariant, strictly anti-monotone in inert capacity
  (the anti-regress), retired only by the key — the sweep found no
  prior meter on the known wall.
- **The unwritable key: KNOWN conclusion, new route.** That
  interpretation cannot be internally encoded or canonically derived
  is among the most-rediscovered results in the literature: Löfgren's
  linguistic complementarity, Harnad's symbol grounding, Kripke's
  rule-following, and above all Putnam's permutation argument — which
  already counts interpretations by permutations, and whose "just more
  theory" reply is exactly the meta-register tower. The
  factorial-vs-polynomial cardinality gap and the tower-level
  equivariance no-go are presented as a new, finite, exact proof route
  to that old conclusion.
- **Participation semantics: prefigured, then made exact.** Wheeler's
  participatory universe, Rovelli's relational facts, and QBism
  prefigure the reading; branch growth from coupled memories appears
  in Everettian toy models. The two exact theorems — the strict
  history surplus of coupling (measurement mints) and the key-free =
  co-minted dichotomy — have, per the sweep, no prior formal
  statement. **Null-result information is presented as an exact
  recovery of Renninger (1960) and Elitzur–Vaidman (1993),** with its
  classical inference-by-elimination character stated plainly.
- **First-person theorems: mixed, honestly.** The biography theorem is
  the union of two classic moves — Everett's memory sequences and
  Lamport's happened-before partition (with Pratt's partial-order
  concurrency drawing the relativity analogy) — cited as such. The
  monotone healing of ignorance with observer size has quantum
  Darwinism's fragment curves as its known shape (Zurek;
  Blume-Kohout–Zurek). **The juxtaposition is the novelty the sweep
  found nowhere: inert capacity worsens the deficit while
  participation heals it — one model, both regimes, exact.** The
  privacy-unit observation (an exterior needs a pair) is flagged as
  plausibly new but near-trivial given the pair-contact model: nobody
  had the model, not the difficulty.
- **Intersubjectivity: a classical recovery, presented against the
  quantum backdrop.** The four-sector partition, forced agreement on
  co-witnessed order, and reconstruction-from-projections are
  textbook facts of Mazurkiewicz trace theory and Zielonka's
  asynchronous automata — cited wholesale, claimed as nothing. The
  *framing* earns its place: the floor forces agreement (no Rashomon)
  exactly where quantum theory's own intersubjectivity is contested
  (Frauchiger–Renner-style arguments; Ozawa's intersubjectivity
  theorem as the QM-side agreement result), and quantum Darwinism's
  consensus results are the physics-side relatives.
- **The balance sheet: demoted to bookkeeping at the sweep's demand.**
  The zero-slack identity is Shannon's chain rule in counting form
  and is presented as accounting, not a result; what is claimed rides
  entirely on the deficit's proven properties (invariance,
  anti-monotonicity, key-retirement). The surrounding thesis has its
  lineage cited: Löfgren, Rosen's closure to efficient causation,
  and the injected-capital reading; the one plausibly new element —
  the capital is a *computed constant* of the world — inherits the
  price structure's novelty.

Nearest single reference to the whole program: Wolpert (2008), missing
only the exact price arithmetic and the participation semantics —
which are, accordingly, what this chapter claims.
