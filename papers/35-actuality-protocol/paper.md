## The door, restated

Chapter 32 proved that coat-following frequencies require an actuality
measure at the record level and that the choice is a received input. Three
candidates stand: **counting** (the uniform path measure — classical
statistics), the **linear coat** |amp| (a quasiprobability-flavored reading;
the Kirkwood–Dirac lineage was engaged in Chapter 32), and the **squared
coat** |amp|². Chapter 34 showed composition does not discriminate among
them. This chapter builds the discrimination suite that does.

## 1. Counting is the macrorealist candidate (recovery)

For every slit configuration in the census (10,242 channels), the
unread-protocol counting distribution equals the read-protocol marginal
exactly: counting satisfies **no-signaling-in-time** identically. This is
the model's exact instance of a known characterization — an additive
(grade-1) measure passes all NSIT conditions, the Leggett–Garg non-invasive
side and the Kofler–Brukner NSIT formulation, with the Clemente–Kofler
analysis of NSIT-based macrorealism the governing frame. Cited as recovery.

## 2. The sign separation

The discriminating structure is the **sign** of NSIT violations:

| candidate | NSIT violations | mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| counting | identically zero | additivity |
| linear coat | never positive (0 / 2547 negative) | triangle inequality: forgetting only darkens |
| squared coat | both signs (783 positive / 513 negative) | constructive fringes exist |

Zero / one-signed / two-signed: an exact three-way discriminator. **A single
positive NSIT violation kills counting- and linear-actuality at once.**

Honesty about altitude: the counting and squared rows are recoveries (Sorkin
grade structure; the squared row is the fringe phenomenon itself). The
linear row's mathematics is one line — a 1-norm measure is subadditive, so
its coarse-graining defects are one-signed — and we present the trichotomy
as an organizing corollary and a usable kill switch, not as a deep theorem.
The nearest engaged relative is Halliwell's quasi-probability program for
Leggett–Garg tests, where negativity of a two-time quasi-probability is the
macrorealism witness. And a disambiguation the terminology invites: our
"linear coat" |amp| is *not* Goldstein–Page linear positivity — that
condition is Re D(α, ᾱ) ≥ 0, linear in the class operator, a different
object; Chapter 31's anchoring of that lineage is unchanged.

## 3. The triple test

Across all depth-2 protocols: the squared coat's third-order Sorkin term
I₃ = 0 exactly on every channel triple — grade 2, quantum's grade (Sorkin's
theorem, the Sinha et al. triple-slit experiments; cited as recovery); the
linear coat's I₃ ≠ 0, witnessed across protocols; counting's I₃ = 0. So a
triple-slit-type experiment separates linear from squared, and NSIT sign
separates counting from both.
