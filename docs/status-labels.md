# Status Labels

Status labels state how a public claim should be read. They are not prestige
markers. A stronger label means a narrower burden has been paid and named.

## Labels

- `THEOREM`: proved at the stated scope, with assumptions and dependencies
  explicit.
- `MODEL-SCOPE`: demonstrated inside a named finite model, carrier, or
  construction. It does not claim full generality.
- `MEASURED`: numerically or exhaustively verified at stated scope, with
  controls or must-fail cases named. It is weaker than a general theorem and
  stronger than a loose illustration.
- `RECOVERY`: reconstructs known mathematics or physics inside the
  finite-contact frame. It is evidence for fit, not novelty by itself.
- `EXTENSION`: an internally new or nonstandard claim with supporting checks,
  but not yet established outside the stated scope.
- `OPEN`: a named hinge, obligation, conjecture, missing proof, or dependency.
- `WITHDRAWN`: a prior claim that failed a gate, audit, control, or wording
  standard. It remains visible for the correction record.
- `NOTHING`: a proposed object, primitive, or noun that the theory dissolves
  rather than recovers.
- `SCHEMA`: a rule, grammar, or classification pattern rather than a completed
  theorem about a particular domain.
- `ADMISSION DATA`: a declared knob, interface, completion, product, parameter,
  or carrier choice that the current theory uses but does not force.

## Qualifiers

Qualifiers may be paired with a label when they prevent overreading:

- `CONDITIONAL`: true only if the named rule, interface, or admission data is
  granted.
- `FRONTIER`: active theorem target or research hinge, not yet public-settled.
- `INTERNAL`: useful inside the framework but not nature-facing.
- `LIMITED`: valid at a narrowed scope or scale.

## Use Rules

- Every public claim must have one primary label.
- Use the weakest accurate label when evidence is mixed.
- A label applies only to the written scope. Changing the scope can change the
  label.
- `THEOREM`, `MODEL-SCOPE`, `MEASURED`, and `RECOVERY` require enough context
  for a reader to find the assumptions, proof, model, measurement, or
  verification path.
- `EXTENSION` and `OPEN` must name what would promote, demote, or kill the
  claim.
- `WITHDRAWN` claims are not deleted from the public record when they have
  already shaped public wording.

## Holds

`HOLD` is not a claim label. It is a publication gate for material whose
evidence, rights posture, wording, or dependency chain is not ready for public
release.

The relevant private work has now closed. Some formerly held claims may
enter public drafting at scoped strength, especially the binary-Bell native
carrier lift. Other items remain held because they are still general-selector,
cross-site, q >= 3, or verification-packaging questions. A hold does not freeze
the claim: it may be revised, split, promoted, demoted, or withdrawn as the
dependency resolves.
