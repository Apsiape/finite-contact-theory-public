# Release Notes — v0.6.0

**Finite Contact Theory v0.6: A Measured Generative Floor.**

Sixth public release; the fifth chapter shipped under the
[EVOLUTION.md](EVOLUTION.md) charter and the
[chapter directory convention](papers/README.md).

## The release ceiling

The live (v0.2) release ceiling is **unchanged** — it continues to control
the README and the claim register. Chapter 6 states its own ceiling in its
opening section: measured facts about a fully specified generative
dynamics, each with a pre-stated quantitative fence asserted by the
shipped script — the program's *experimental* face at public scope.

## What is new

- **Chapter 6**:
  [papers/06-measured-floor/](papers/06-measured-floor/paper.md) — the
  repository's first shipped *instrument*: a complete, dependency-free
  public floor engine (the driven one-use dynamics, specified exactly),
  with a replay facility and a maximally-coupled counterfactual mode —
  plus three measurements of it:
  1. **delayed individuation** — the drive manufactures twins
     (indistinguishable constituents) and 92.7% separate before world end
     (censoring 7.3%); the private laboratory engine, a different
     codebase at different sizes, measured 0.927/0.911 — an
     independent-implementation agreement on the headline number;
  2. **short-tailed separation** — twin-separation waits are
     exponential-form: geometric beats the best power law by ΔLL = 67.8
     (fence: > 10);
  3. **ballistic counterfactual defects** — redirect one fork choice under
     an identical random stream and the edge-set defect grows *linearly*
     (slope 1.25 edges/add, R² = 0.998): one changed contact neither heals
     nor explodes — it propagates at a constant counted rate.
- Claim rows **FCT-38 through FCT-41** (one `MODEL / INSTRUMENT`, three
  `MEASURED / MODEL-SCOPE`); theorem rows **T-30 through T-32**; results
  ledger
  [FCT-38-FCT-41-measured-floor-RESULTS.md](verification/results/FCT-38-FCT-41-measured-floor-RESULTS.md).
- Shipped verification: `verification/scripts/floor_engine_measurements.py`
  (stdlib-only, ≈3 s, fences meant to survive seed changes), wired into
  `run_all.py`, with a frozen copy and verbatim expected output in the
  chapter directory.

## Negative result carried honestly

The **redundancy-engine inversion** (the driven order individuates slower
than its own shuffled null; private scale n = 1600, sign test p ≈ 2⁻²⁷) is
cited and **explicitly not claimed**: the public-scale probe (n ≤ 64) is
null, and the chapter reports that scale fence instead of the claim.

## Scope fences carried in this release

- Measured facts about the specified dynamics only; nothing about nature.
- No n-scaling laws, no defect-radius measurement, no parameter sweeps
  beyond the shipped weights.
