## Proof Sketch Stubs

These sketches are public orientation only. Full private proofs or scripts are
cited where they are not yet shipped.

### T-01 No-Totality For The Open Fresh-Mark Rule

The open rule contains a fresh-mark act from every nonempty finite fragment and
a seed act from the empty fragment. Since the fresh act introduces a mark not
already in the support, it is not a reuse of an old act. Thus every reachable
finite fragment has at least one legal extension. The shipped script enumerates
reachable labeled fragments through depth 7 and finds zero jams. Capped rules
are separate admission data and can jam.

### T-02 Integrality Blocks Native Colimits

The native site contains finite fragments and finite morphisms. A completed
colimit/totality would require identifying a finished object that represents
all future extensions. Under the open fresh-mark branch, T-01 blocks such a
terminal object. Under the finite-contact posture, completed totalities enter
only as admitted overlays or external completions.

### T-03 Frequency Bridge

For a fixed finite fork, micro-presentations are counted by multiplicity. Types
with empirical frequencies far from the counting shares can be enumerated
exactly. The standard finite type bound gives an exponential upper bound on
their count-share. The shipped script verifies the exact exchangeable core for
the fork with multiplicities `(2,2,1)`. The growing-floor compensator theorem
remains cited from the private research corpus.

### T-04 Born As Counting Through Gluing

At rational scope, split an event into a finite exclusive witness fiber whose
cardinality realizes the rational weight. One-receiver gluing pushes witness
counts forward to received event weights. Consistency requires that refinement
does not change the received behavior. The private research corpus verifies this
two-way consistency at scope; irrational weights are handled by the `[REAL]`
completion posture, not by finite exact counts.

### T-04b Witness-Consumption Decomposition

The refinement sub-events are not abstract labels. They are modeled as
witness consumptions in a floor fork. Sibling consumptions are exclusive by
one-use, and pushed-forward witness counts reproduce the received event
weights. Degenerate bare-witness cases and alien floors remain outside this
public row.

### T-05 CHSH-Family Boundary / Pell Fence

In the CHSH-family construction, finite rational partners approach the
`2*sqrt(2)` boundary. A Pell-form sign test decides whether a rational rung
sits below or above the boundary. This is host-free at rational scope but still
depends on named product/inclusion assumptions for broader generality.

### T-05b CHSH Floor Arena

A finite gated floor fragment realizes the CHSH event/exclusivity graph. This
establishes the arena, not the super-classical weights. Flat witness-counted
weights remain classical in the public scope; the weight/magic bridge is
frontier.

### T-06 Native Carrier Grammar

Binary one-use forks produce idempotent sibling projectors. Their signed
difference is an involution. Recurrent restaging supplies multiplication,
site-disjointness supplies tensoring, and stationary/reversible readout supplies
the positive-state sector in the private research corpus. The shipped script verifies
the exact involution and factorization cores; the rest of the private research corpus remains cited.

### T-07 Witness-Overlap Complementarity

For two setting families sharing `k` of `n` witnesses, shared witnesses
contribute one unit of alignment and unshared complementary witnesses contribute
zero at the readout. Normalized witness counting gives `t(k,n)=k/n`. The
shipped script verifies this exactly for the public finite families `n=4` and
`n=6`.

### T-08 Binary-Bell Quantum Boundary

The native lift makes the relevant binary-Bell carrier floor-grown rather than
imported. Existing finite tensor certificates can therefore bind that carrier
at the stated scope. This licenses the scoped sentence "the quantum boundary is
a floor theorem at binary-Bell finite carrier scope." It does not license a
general derivation of all quantum mechanics.

### T-08a Through T-08d Carrier Algebra And Sliwa Caps

The finite two-involution and projective-binary tensor rows are algebraic
carrier results. Sliwa-23 and Sliwa-41 then serve as finite tensor separator
certificates at their scopes. These are recovery/certificate rows: they become
floor-relevant because T-06 grows the carrier, not because this release
re-derives all certificate theory.

### T-09 Carrier States And Temperature

In the tested recurrent-chain family, positive carrier readout appears exactly
at the detailed-balance sector. The the private research corpus identifies
visible temperature at zero visible entropy production. The private result
therefore matches carrier-state existence and visible temperature at measured
scope. This remains measured, not proved generally.

### T-10 Receiver Is Not An Agent

The receiver is modeled as a gluing/co-reception condition over contexts. Once
the gluing class is specified, no extra mind or selector is needed to define
the received structure. This dissolves the receiver as primitive agent while
leaving actual observers as later aperture/reception structures.

### T-11 Mathematics As Stream Erasure

The mathematical phase keeps invariant structure and erases the serial which.
This explains why law-sector structure can survive erasure while indexical
facts do not. The private stream-erasure pair carries the detailed result.

### T-12 Continuum As Admitted Completion

Finite predictions use finite, algebraic, rational, or computable horizons.
Completed continuum objects are admitted overlays unless their predictive
content is reconstructed finitely. This is a placement theorem/program, not a
solution to the interacting-QFT continuum limit.

### T-13 Aperture-Level Energy And Temperature

Energy and temperature are not introduced at the primitive one-use floor. In
the tested visible sectors, temperature appears when visible entropy production
vanishes. Gravity-facing sourcing remains open; the public row is an aperture
thermodynamics result, not a derivation of gravity.
