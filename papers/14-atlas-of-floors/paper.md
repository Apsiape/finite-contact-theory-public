## 0. The question this chapter answers

The program's charter is a *taxonomy of inevitability*: sort what a
generative floor **forces** from what it merely **receives**. Chapters 1–13
built one floor's derivation stack. This chapter asks the charter question
itself: **was any of it inevitable?** If every generative floor grew the
same structures, the reconstruction would be a tautology; if no two floors
shared anything, it would be biography. The honest answer is a chart.

The method: build genuinely rival floors — a constraint-refinement floor
with no marks, a coagulation floor whose irreversibility points the
opposite way, a reversible floor, a counter floor — and measure all of
them with the same two capability questions:

- **Order-writability (OW):** can the order of two acts be written into
  the persisting state?
- **Residue-retention (RR):** can a *formally repeatable* act (idempotent
  or involutive by specification) leave "used twice" distinguishable
  in-state from "used once"?

## 1. The axes are real, independent — and presentation-relative

Two exact facts discipline everything (both shipped as checks):

**RR cannot live in a bare act system.** A genuine action satisfies every
equation of its acting monoid — an actual idempotent map cannot
distinguish twice from once. Residue-retention lives in the gap between a
**formal content specification** and the **realized history action**: RR
holds exactly when the history action fails to factor through the formal
specification. The minimal example is startling: a formally *idempotent*
operation realized as a toggle retains multiplicity on a **fixed
two-element state space** — retention requires no growth, no event tokens,
only the specification/realization gap.

**So the classification is of *presented observed floors*.** This is a
scope statement, not a defect — and for generative floors it is natural:
a floor's own dynamics designates its act equations ("the same mark, used
twice"), and its measurement discipline designates its content map (this
program's counting-observable rule, adopted at the founding for gauge
reasons, *is* that map). What a floor retains depends on what it treats
as content.

The axes are logically independent: all four (OW, RR) combinations occur
on state spaces of size at most two. They are also *not complete*:
unbounded orbit growth is a third independent axis, and growth-rate
refinements continue indefinitely. The atlas is open in that direction,
and says so.

## 2. The four cells, with their fences shipped

| | **scar-free** | **residue-retaining** |
|---|---|---|
| **order-blind** | lattice floors (refinement, coagulation) | commutative creation (counters) |
| **order-writable** | reversible floors (group actions) | **noncommuting creation (marks)** |

Each cell theorem ships with the counterexample that fences it:

- **Lattice floors (00):** meet-acts with a *justification-stable*
  propagation closure have order-independent settled states — verified
  over hundreds of random constraint systems, staged versus joint closure
  exactly equal. The fence: this is a **terminal-semantics** theorem, and
  the stability condition is necessary — two non-commuting *interior*
  operators (pure monotone descent!) write order, and the counterexample
  is in the script. Coagulation (partition join) is orderless by the same
  lattice mechanism, exhaustively over all orderings of merge-sets.
- **Reversible floors (10):** order-writability is exactly
  noncommutativity of the realized transformation image; and a genuine
  group action can never scar against its own full-state equations —
  `g² = id` returns the state with certainty. Retention on a reversible
  floor requires an external ledger. This cell is the natural home of the
  reversibility-axiom reconstructions of quantum theory.
- **Commutative creation (01):** enriched counters over an idempotent
  content specification retain multiplicity with no order.
- **Marks (11):** Chapter 13's retained contact has both — and its
  first-order scar is *minimal* retention: one central bit.

**Quotient monotonicity, in its safe scope:** equivariant quotients with
fixed equation designations can only destroy capabilities; the transitions
11→01 (forget order, keep counts) and 11→10 (normalize the residue, keep
order) are both realized in the script. The honest boundary is also
shipped: a quotient can *create an equation designation* (the integer
successor becomes a parity involution downstairs), so residue-monotonicity
requires the presentation to be held fixed.

**The boundary layer** (measured on the research corpus's engines, cited):
a priced-reversal floor is scar-free exactly when its cost never binds —
the reversible cell is the infinite-budget limit of a scarred floor, with
the scar pushed past the horizon rather than erased; and marginalizing a
creating floor's record sector returns its statistics to the scar-free
cells — how classical a world looks is how many records the description
keeps.

satisfy `f² = f♯² = 0` and `ff♯ + f♯f = 1` **exactly**. Every

## 4. The retention tower, computed through third order

Retention does not stop at one bit. In the presented-floor formalization,
first-order scars of an involutive specification with a central two-element
kernel are classified by the second cohomology of the contact symmetry —
and the tower continues: second-order scars (retention of the *composing*
of scars — the associator) live in third cohomology; third-order scars in
fourth. The shipped script computes the full censuses for the four-mark
chiral contact group by exact linear algebra over the two-element field:

| rung | group | meaning | census |
|---|---|---|---|
| H² | C₂ | first-order scar | **unique** — the double cover of Chapter 13 |
| H³ | C₂ × C₂ | second-order (associator) | four classes, all pentagon-coherent, none gauge-removable |
| H⁴ | C₂ | third-order | **exactly one** nontrivial class |

Two operational results ride along, both shipped:

- **The bracket witness.** The value `ω(g,g,g)` — the defect between the
  `((gg)g)` and `(g(gg))` parenthesizations of a triple self-contact of an
  involutive act — is **gauge-invariant** (the coboundary contribution
  cancels identically on that cycle) and **reads** the second-order
  classes: one nontrivial class answers loud, one silent. Parenthesization
  is physically real on tower-bearing floors, and the reading protocol is
  one bit.
- **Free-asphericity.** A pentagon *defect* manufactured from any
  ill-chosen associator is its own coboundary — removable by
  redefinition, always. Incoherent bracketing can never certify genuine
  third-order structure: that requires realizing the unique H⁴ class.
  This turns a previously open program question — could pentagon-violating
  identification structure be a divergence class? — into a *targeted* one:
  there is exactly one mathematical object to realize or exclude.

## 5. The measurement obstruction, relocated and runnable

The deepest consequence runs on 24 elements, entirely in the shipped
script. Take `E` = the double cover (the history space, where the retained
bit `z` lives) and `L` = its quotient (the law space, reversible group
dynamics), with `q: E → L` the covering map. Then, exactly:

- **No equivariant section exists** — all 4096 lift-choices are tested;
  none is a homomorphism. Any *law-defined outcome selector* would be
  exactly such a section. **The obstruction to a law that picks outcomes
  and the floor's central no-selector law are the same fact.**
- **Complete law-level tomography is scar-blind**, while the double action
  of any involutive law element flips the retained bit with certainty —
  a *tomographically silent echo*: the law calls the two histories
  identical; a scar reader separates them every time.
- **Registration promotion is quantified:** the best of all 4096 law-only
  prediction rules for the retained bit scores exact chance (12/24);
  promoting the read bit into the law-state gives certainty (24/24).

The reading, stated at model scope: a reversible law over a retaining
history space is *complete as law and incomplete as history*. Asking the
law to produce the outcome is asking for a section that provably does not
exist — a type error, not a dynamical puzzle. What changes at a
registration is not the law vector but *which lift is actual and what the
next law-state may condition on*. This is a structural observation about
presented floors, not a claim about nature; its nature-facing form (could
any physical scar reader separate what state-complete tomography calls
identical?) is named in the roadmap as an open question with heavy
controls.

  relocation model. Every nature-facing echo of these structures is an

- The capability axes are **presentation-relative** (a theorem, not a

  continue). The atlas is a chart of what floors *can carry*, not a
  complete coordinate system.
- The cell theorems carry their exact fences: the lattice theorem is

  is relative to full-state equations; quotient monotonicity requires
  fixed designations.
- The boundary-layer measurements (priced reversal; record
  marginalization) are cited from the research corpus, not shipped.
- **What would demote it:** a floor retaining against its own *full-state*
  equations without any enrichment (would break the RR theorem); a
  capability created by an equation-preserving equivariant quotient
  (would break monotonicity); an error in the censuses (all three are
  recomputable from the shipped script in seconds).
