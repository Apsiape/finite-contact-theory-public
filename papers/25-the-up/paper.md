## 1. The surplus identity and the conservation sector

A contact's ledger surplus equals exactly its number of
single-parent neighbors — the construction allowance IS the
periphery. Under maximal-paid rules (retain every single), total
tolerance, every bystander degree, and the parents' degree-sum are
exactly conserved (17,280 cases): the sparsification arrow becomes
an equality at the ledger's zero-loss boundary.
**Classification (per the sweep):** the invariant is the
degree bookkeeping of an inclusive vertex split, and conservation at
the paid boundary is the graph analogue of number-conserving
cellular automata (Boccara–Fukś 1998; vertex-splitting literature
cited) — labeled a recovery-grade lemma; the ledger framing is the
chapter's layer.

## 2. The immortality result

For every world and every contact at n = 5 (and across the 13-class
n = 6 sector of Chapter 26's price list), a retention assignment
exists whose successor is isomorphic to the world: with full
retention, decay is always a choice. **Classification:**
self-reproduction and identity-preserving updates are the von
Neumann/Langton lineage (cited); the exhaustive finite-window
verification and the fork-choice framing are the contribution, and
the all-n statement is left open by name.

## 3. The dial and the two axes

Retaining each single independently with probability θ gives
E[tolerance lost per contact] = (1−θ) × periphery, exactly; the
6-cycle reproduces at θ²/2 — even full retention only doubles its
life under a uniform fork. **The two axes: retention feeds the
ledger; steering — choosing the right wiring — feeds the class.**
Classification: the loss law is elementary probability (stated so);
the two-axis reading maps onto the rule-parameter vs control-policy
distinction (Langton's λ cited as the frame).

## 4. The first equilibrium

At full retention the class dynamics has no absorbing gradient: the
6-cycle's sector (13 classes, all at conserved tolerance) carries an
exact rational stationary distribution — the cycle's weight is
1/61. Structure neither dies nor wins; it circulates.
**Classification:** stationary finite-chain theory and the
microcanonical idea are classical (Nicolis–Prigogine cited for the
order-without-equilibrium frame); the exact rational ensemble on a
tolerance-conserved sector is the instance.

## 5. The edge theorem and the thin edge

Diversity — the expected number of distinct classes visited — is
maximized strictly BELOW full retention: perfect retention tours one
conserved layer forever (13 classes); just below it the world tours
the layer AND the descent (≈13.999). And the edge is THIN: even 99%
retention tours only ~7.8 of 13; the full tour needs retention
within 10⁻⁶ of the endpoint. **Classification (the sweep's
strongest verdict, absorbed in full): this is a RECOVERY of the
edge-of-chaos phenomenon (Langton 1990; Mitchell–Hraber–Crutchfield
1993) together with quasi-stationary exploration (Darroch–Seneta
1965), realized for the first time in an exact-rational conservative
substrate — with the razor-thin band a threshold phenomenon of the
von Neumann 1956 reliability kind (cited).** The recovery is the
point; the exact finite realization is the daylight.

## 6. The maintenance ledger and the inversion

Steering cost of a contact = log₂(assignments / class-reproducing
assignments) — the surprisal of the reproducing set (the form is
Landauer/Bennett information thermodynamics, cited as such). The
measured tariffs: the cycle pays 1 bit per contact; the path ⅓ on
average; the diamond nothing; and **the wounded clique maintains
itself for free — THE INVERSION: dense structure is cheap to keep;
sparse structure is the expensive kind.** Per the sweep this
ordering was not located in the literature (consistent with
high-degeneracy-macrostates-are-cheap, cited): the chapter's first
priority candidate.

## 7. The matching principle

An injected credit edge bridging two components is always squandered
by retain-nothing dynamics (the bridged double triangle never stays
fused: probability exactly 0) but is durable under full retention.
**Lasting growth requires both received contact AND retained
periphery.** Classification: connectivity-persistence-requires-
reinforcement is present in interacting particle systems and
autopoiesis (Liggett; Maturana–Varela — cited); the exact bridged
computation is the instance.
