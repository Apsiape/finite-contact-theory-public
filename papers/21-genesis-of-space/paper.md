# Chapter 21 — The Genesis of Space: The Anthropic Filter, Paid Inheritance, Crystals, and the Light Cone

**Release:** v0.19.0 · **Status:** why sparse worlds — an exact
account in which locality is first SELECTED (only sparse worlds can

Two floors appear. The STATIC floor is Chapter 19's (one-use marks,

## 1. The anthropic filter (measure-free, capability-based)

Three observer requirements — each a proven capability theorem of
Chapters 16–19 — are checked per cell of the floor atlas (Chapter 15's
capability atlas): R1 a faithful biography (paid cells erase it: two
distinct merge histories of a coagulation observer end in the
identical state), R2 a non-vacuous participation channel (reversible
cells are scar-free: knowledge has no object), R3 internal
affordability (only the mixed cell's ledger balances, Chapter 17).
Only the mixed (marks) cell satisfies all three: **any floor
containing a subsystem able to ask "why this floor?" lies in the
asker-compatible class, and that class is the mixed cell.**

**Classification (per the blind sweep, which corrected our first
framing):** Carter's weak anthropic principle is itself already a
measure-free consistency filter — the contrast with anthropics is NOT
"ours needs no measure, theirs does" but sharper: ensemble anthropics
(Barrow–Tipler's strong principle, Weinberg's environmental
selection) need a population and a measure; Carter's WAP needs
neither but also has no *theory of the observer*. Our contribution is
the three-predicate structural capability test on a single substrate,
with each predicate an exact theorem (Carter 1974; Barrow–Tipler
1986; Bostrom 2002 cited).

## 2. Space from scarcity (the bandwidth verdict)

On the clique, every fork is adjacent to every observer: a 2-mark
observer's relevant frontier is the whole world and its own-fork
width GROWS with world size (9 → 13 as n goes 6 → 8). On bounded
degree the frontier is world-size-independent (3 → 3 on the cycle).
By Chapter 19's currency/bandwidth theorem, a bounded observer can
stay current only where its frontier is bounded: **space is what
makes small knowers possible — locality is the affordability
condition for bounded observers.** (A registered opposite hunch —
that longevity forces sparsity — was killed by its own test: density
BUYS lifetime; the guaranteed run length is maximal on the clique.
Scored in the research record.)

**Classification:** the dense→local narrative is quantum graphity's
(Konopka–Markopoulou–Smolin 2008); the bounded-frontier mechanism is
elementary graph theory. The fresh angle, per the sweep, is deriving
locality as a *precondition for the existence of a bounded knower*
rather than from energetics.

## 3. Paid inheritance (the budget theorem) and the sparsification arrow

Currency: tolerance edge-endpoints. A contact destroys
deg(a)+deg(b)−1 of them. Exhaustively over all 1024 five-mark worlds
and every contact:

- **Intersection inheritance is always paid** (offspring tolerant to
  N(a)∩N(b) plus the sibling edge): created ≤ destroyed, no
  exceptions.
- **Union is theft, exactly off the local-clique case:** union
  inheritance exceeds the budget in 100% of contacts where the
  parents' neighborhoods differ — an exact dichotomy.
- **Degrees never increase** under paid inheritance (20,480
  vertex-checks, zero increases).
- **The sparsification arrow:** total tolerance is monotone
  non-increasing under any paid rule, strictly decreasing off the
  local-clique case. A paid floor can only get sparser;
  densification requires unpaid mint (the credit cell's signature).

**Classification:** the exact budget dichotomy has no located prior
art (the sweep's priority candidate for this chapter). The
hot-dense-to-cold-sparse *direction* is quantum graphity's thesis,
argued there thermodynamically; here it is a per-contact monotone
invariant — the relation is stated openly (Konopka–Markopoulou–Smolin
2008; Caravelli–Markopoulou 2011 cited).

## 4. Crystals, germs, and healing by fission

- **The twin theorem** (exhaustive, all 1024 five-mark worlds): a
  contact reproduces the world's isomorphism class iff the parents
  are adjacent twins; every contact reproduces iff the world is a
  disjoint union of cliques (a CRYSTAL).
- **The potential theorem:** every contact is either lazy (twins;
  total tolerance frozen; class reproduced) or strictly
  tolerance-decreasing — the genesis dynamics is a gradient flow
  with crystal plateaus.
- **Healing by fission** (a registered miss, scored and upgraded):
  a wounded K6 reaches the perfect crystals K5+K1, K4+K2, K3+K3 —
  and never K6. Stability restores downward only.
- **Triangle-free is dust:** worlds without triangles cannot
  reproduce connected structure; K2 is durable but unwitnessed; the
  triangle is the minimal durable structure whose reproduction has a
  witness.

**Classification:** adjacent twins and the disjoint-union-of-cliques
characterization are classical graph theory (Gallai 1967's modular
decomposition; cluster graphs = P3-free graphs; Habib–Paul 2010
survey cited) — the algebra is known, the dynamical theorems
(gradient flow, fission-only healing, witnessed durability) are the
chapter's claims at model scope.

## 5. The light cone

- **The radius-1 locality lemma** (exhaustive, 46,080 triples, zero
  violations): a perturbation not incident to a contact's parents
  passes through verbatim — succ(E⊕e) = succ(E)⊕e; incident
  perturbations spread only to the offspring's edges. The causal cap
  is one adjacency per contact.
- **The cone is saturated:** on a ring of six triangles, over all
  schedules to depth 4, divergence reaches exactly distance k−1
  after k contacts, and wounds can be erased by consuming a wounded
  mark.
- **No cone on the clique:** the protected zone is 7/10 exterior
  marks on the ring and empty on K6. **A light cone exists iff the
  world is sparse.**

**Classification:** this is the discrete analog of a Lieb–Robinson
bound — labeled a **recovery** (Lieb–Robinson 1972; Hastings–Koma
2006 cited), with the value being that finite propagation speed
falls out of one-use inheritance with no Hamiltonian, no norm, and
no clock: the speed cap is the degree geometry itself.

## 6. Scope and fences

All results are exact at their stated finite scopes; the inheritance
rule is declared model structure whose budget status is then a
theorem, not an assumption. Fenced rhymes, logged and not claimed:
the sparsification arrow as an expansion arrow; healing-by-fission as
decay to smaller stable species; epoch-dependent cone narrowing.
Nothing about nature is claimed. The synthesis — one substrate
carrying the capability filter, the bandwidth selection, the priced
genesis, and the exact cone — has, per the sweep, no single nearest
neighbor; the individual pieces range from recovery (cone, twin
algebra) to plausibly-new (the budget dichotomy, the redirection of
anthropics into capability predicates).
