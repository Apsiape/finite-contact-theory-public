## 1. The width hierarchy: the qutrit is forced at six photons

Chapter 12 found the n = 5 phase transition: the irrep (3,1,1) acquires
cyclic multiplicity two — an emergent logical qubit from permutation
symmetry and counting alone. The engine that takes this to all n is
classical: the multiplicity of the cyclic character ω^j in the
S_n-irrep λ equals the number of standard Young tableaux of shape λ
with major index ≡ j (mod n) (Kraskiewicz–Weyman; equivalently
Klyachko, Springer, Stembridge; the q = ω^j face of the cyclic sieving
phenomenon of Reiner–Stanton–White — all cited, §4). The shipped
verifier recomputes Chapter 12's commutant dimensions from this
independent method (4, 10, 28, 136, 726, 5100 for n = 3..8, matching
the closed form exactly) and computes the profile:

- **Max fiber width by n: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 12.**
- **The first emergent qutrit is *forced* at n = 6**: the shape (3,2,1)
  has 16 standard tableaux distributed over 6 residues, so some
  multiplicity is at least ⌈16/6⌉ = 3 — a pigeonhole theorem, given
  Kraskiewicz–Weyman. Four M₃ blocks appear, at residues 1, 2, 4, 5
  (notably *not* at residue 0, unlike the n = 5 qubit).
- **The hidden budget explodes:** the commutant dimension invisible to
  single-shot counting is 0, 0, 2, 56, 480, 4290 for n = 3..8 —
  counting sees a vanishing fraction of the emergent algebra.

The growth of these multiplicities is known mathematics (Swanson's
existence and equidistribution theorems — cited, §4); the max-width
table and the pigeonhole instance are computations within that theory.
The *physical* statement — emergent qudits living in the
cyclic-multiplicity spaces of photonic internal-state irreps, with a
forced first qutrit at six photons — has, per the blind sweep, no
prior appearance; the nearest bodies of work are the suppression-law
literature (Dittel et al., which uses the coarse residue grading but
never the within-irrep multiplicities) and the noiseless-subsystem
tradition (logical qudits in multiplicity spaces of a *different*
multiplicity) — both cited as nearest art.

## 2. The exposure law

Chapter 12 proved rebit-blindness at n = 5: single-shot cyclic counts
are blind to σ_y on the qubit fiber, and multiplication recovers it.
The research corpus's fiber engines (discovery grade, cited — the
compressions of all 462 count effects onto every fiber, built from the
Young orthogonal representation) extend this to **every multiplicit
fiber at n = 6** — all 22 of them, across 7 shapes, both widths:

> **The exposure law.** On every multiplicity-m fiber, the single-shot
> count effects span *exactly* the m(m+1)/2-dimensional symmetric part
> — they are Hermitian on the fiber, real-symmetric in a suitable
> basis — and one multiplication layer opens the full m². Counting
> sees the shape of every emergent fiber, never its orientation; the
> orientation is always and only one multiplication away.

The *phenomenon* has honorable ancestors, all cited (§4): the
m(m+1)/2 count is the observable space of real-vector-space quantum
theory (Stueckelberg; Hardy–Wootters); conjugation-blindness of
counting and the cosine-only dependence on collective triad phases are
on record (Dhand et al.; Menssen et al.); "real effects cannot detect
imaginarity" is a theorem of the imaginarity resource theory
(Hickey–Gour; Wu et al.); and sequential protocols recovering
imaginary Bargmann invariants are established (Oszmaniec–Brod–Galvão).
What the sweep could not find anywhere is the *exactness*: that the
compressed effects span exactly — not merely within — the full
symmetric part, on every fiber, fiber-exhaustively. That equality is
what makes the law a law.

## 3. Width, not depth

The hierarchy's resource question — does unlocking the hidden algebra
get *harder* with n? — has a two-part answer:

- **Strict in width:** the number and size of hidden fibers grows
  (qubit at 5, qutrit at 6, width 12 by n = 8; hidden dimensions
  4290 by n = 8).
- **Saturated in depth:** real-symmetric generators plus one
  product/commutator layer span all of M_m for every m (the algebraic
  lemma is elementary/folklore — [sym, sym] = antisym; the rebit-
  recovery tradition of Rudolph–Grover and McKague–Mosca–Gisin is the
  nearest physics — cited). Given the exposure law, one multiplication
  always suffices where the symmetric part is exposed — verified on
  the actual n = 6 qutrit fiber (corpus engines, cited).

> **What grows with n is how many hidden fibers there are and how wide
> — never how deep the algebra must dig.**

Per the sweep, no prior formulation of this width-vs-depth dichotomy
for what sequential composition adds to counting measurements was
found; it is claimed as an organizing law with its lemma flagged as
known.
