# Hold Register

Status: reconciliation gate.

This file separates what may enter a public release at scoped strength
from what still stays private, frontier, or citation-only.

`HOLD` is not a claim label. It is a release decision. A held item may be
important or internally supported, but it is not yet public-load-bearing.

## Required Public Wording

Allowed:

```text
At binary-Bell finite carrier scope, the carrier grammar is generated from
one-use contact, and the standard finite tensor certificates bind a
floor-grown carrier rather than an imported Hilbert host.
```

Not allowed:

```text
The floor derives all of quantum mechanics.
The floor selects the quantum boundary in every scenario.
The almost-quantum problem is solved.
```

The release rule is simple: if the phrase "quantum boundary is a floor theorem"
appears, the phrase "at binary-Bell finite carrier scope" must appear in the
same sentence or the next one.
