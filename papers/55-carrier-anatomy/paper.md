## 1. MaxAbs, formalized — and what it does not force

The carrier discipline is a single operation. An admitted cell, by extensional
closure, *is* its complete lawful tester row — a positive functional ω on the
tester operator system A of the settled doctrine, with tester composition given
by typed partial reentry. A carrier-type debt is an unresolved distinction in
ω: mixedness is the row failing to determine a unique maximal refinement. The
floor pays such a debt by **maximal absorption** — MaxAbs — the debt-blind
universal payment:

- **debt-blind** — the absorbing extension may not inspect the debt's content;
  one uniform construction for the whole staged fiber (the no-selector law
  applied to absorption), so the payment is *natural* in ω;
- **maximal / universal** — it recovers every lawful decomposition of the debt;
  any positive-carrier realization of ω factors through it.

The temptation, and the program's first overclaim here, was to read MaxAbs as
purification: a universal debt-blind payment *must* realize ω as a vector
(pure) state on an enlarged carrier, uniquely up to gauge, and every cell-map
as an isometric dilation — GNS and Stinespring falling straight out of the
incorporation-canonicity clause. That reading is **false as stated**, and the
countermodel is elementary.

**The classical-copy countermodel.** Let the payment be the copy carrier

  Ψ(a, e) = x_a · δ_{a,e},

which pairs each classical alternative a of weight x_a with a fresh
distinguishing token e. Ψ is debt-blind (it inspects no content — it copies),
it is universal (it recovers *every* decomposition of the classical mixture),
and it is *mixed with no pure classical extension*: a diagonal cannot be made
extremal by enlarging a diagonal. Universality does not force purity. The GNS
route silently assumed the admitted carrier class already contained the full
GNS representation — which is exactly the coherence question it was meant to
settle. So the honest headline is:

  **MaxAbs alone does not imply purification.**

The purity content lives one gate deeper, and the rest of this section locates
that gate exactly.

## 2. The purity theorem, corrected — the Key Fact and the irreducible face

Fix the composite payment under a named hypothesis:

- **(H_A)** — the composite cell sits inside the maximal tensor product of its
  parts; equivalently, partial evaluation against an environment effect is
  positive. This is not an extra axiom about nature; it is the definition of a
  *lawful* composite, and it is where the argument's positivity enters.

Consider a **minimal** universal payment T of a mixed cell: the smallest
environment that steers every lawful decomposition. The court-discovered fact
that reorganizes the whole theorem is a linear-algebra triviality that neither
the original proof nor its first adversary saw:

  **The Key Fact (minimal ⟹ faithful steering).** For a minimal payment,
  steering is faithful: ker T = 0 on effects, and by linearity this extends to
  the whole composite. "Invisible to all environment steering" therefore means
  *identically zero*. The recovery-null residue — the part of a payment that no
  environment measurement can see — is not small; for a minimal payment it does
  not exist.

This collapses the earlier, baroque "central-broadcast residue XOR
recovery-null holistic residue" split into a clean dichotomy on the face F

  **trivial decomposition   XOR   broadcast splits F.**

Either the payment admits only the trivial (one-piece) decomposition — it is
extremal, i.e. pure — or the face F itself carries a genuine superselection
split, with an environment-readable, perfectly correlated sector label. There
is no third possibility, because the would-be third (a holistic recovery-null
residue) is zero by the Key Fact. The broadcast horn is *stronger* than the
program originally claimed: it is not a scalar ambiguity but an actual split of
F, E-readable and perfectly correlated.

From this the corrected theorem reads in two clauses.

**The Irreducible-Face Purity Theorem (unconditional).** If the face F is
irreducible — no nontrivial superselection split — then every minimal

machinery is used; the whole content is the Key Fact plus (H_A). This is the
jewel of the anatomy, and it is unconditional at finite scope.

**The reducible verdict (posit-relative).** If F is reducible — a genuine
classical sector split — then universality *forces* the broadcast state (the

  **DNR (Distinction Non-Rehypothecation), at receipt-individuated ledger
