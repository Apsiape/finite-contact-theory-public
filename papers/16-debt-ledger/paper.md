## 0. The question

Five times, in five different guises, the program has met the number
`ceil(log2 n)`: the selector-cohomology debt rank, the actualization debt
of a commissioned blind analysis, the record count of Chapter 14's
registration promotion, the erasure bill of the thermodynamics literature,
and the garbage registers of reversible computation. This chapter asks the
question the coincidence demands: **is there a conservation law?** The
answer is yes, it is exact, and it needs nothing but counting.

Throughout, `maxfiber(f) = max_t |f^{-1}(t)|` (the largest preimage
multiplicity of an act `f` on a finite state set `S`), and the **price** of
an act is `log2 maxfiber(f)`.

## 1. Dilation: what irreversibility costs, exactly

Three exact facts (script Section 1):

**No fixed carrier absorbs a merge.** A non-injective act admits *no* lift
to a permutation of `S × A` whose first component is `f` on the whole
product, for any finite ancilla `A` — a counting fact (a fiber of size
`m ≥ 2` would need `m·|A|` slots in `|A|`). Lifts correct on a single
ancilla slice exist, but fail under composition — the classic garbage
lesson, exhibited by a minimal exact counterexample.

**A fresh register per step always suffices.** With one fresh one-use
register per act, every act monoid dilates reversibly and compositionally
(history separation within fibers). This is the founding construction of
reversible computation (Lecerf 1963; Bennett 1973), recomputed as a check.

**The exchange rate is exactly maxfiber.** The minimal fresh-register
alphabet for an exact injective lift `s ↦ (f(s), c(s))` is exactly
`maxfiber(f)` — verified exhaustively. In bit form this is the
minimal-garbage formula of the reversible-circuit literature
(Maslov–Dueck 2003/04: minimal garbage = `ceil(log2 q)`, `q` the maximum
output-pattern multiplicity); the program registered this as an open
question and the blind sweep answered it with a citation — a clean
recovery. Hence: **the n-fork collapse act (n states to one) prices at
exactly `log2 n` fresh bits.**

The price composes lawfully: `maxfiber(g∘f) ≤ maxfiber(g)·maxfiber(f)`
(subadditive debt along histories, strictness witnessed), and permutation
acts keep price 1 under every respected quotient — while an
absorbing-state notion of "mortality" is discontinuous under the same
quotients, the act-price sees through them.

## 2. The ledger

**The ledger inequality** (exact, exhaustive on small cases plus random
histories, multiplicative form):

```
log2 |S|  <=  log2 |im w(S)|  +  Σ_k price(f_k)
```

for any history `w = f_k ∘ … ∘ f_1`. Equality is achieved (aligned uniform
merges), and can fail strictly in one instructive way: a merge can re-pay
for distinction already destroyed — **waste is possible; theft is
impossible. Distinction is never destroyed unpaid.** The entropy form
`H(f(X)) ≥ H(X) − log2 maxfiber(f)` and its support form are the standard
grouping bound (labeled RECOVERY, §4) — Landauer's counting face.

**One number, three readings.** For the n-fork, verified independently for
`n = 2…16`: the dilation register alphabet, the record alphabet needed for
registration-promotion certainty (Chapter 14's promotion, priced), and the
optimal binary-question identification depth are one number — `n` in
alphabet form, `ceil(log2 n)` in binary form. **The cost to collapse the
fork, the cost to know the outcome, and the cost to remember it are the
same ledger entry.** The `ceil` marks exactly the binary-resource
convention; the alphabet forms are exact.

**The floor reading.** The program's central law is that the floor stages
forks and forces no selector. The ledger makes that law quantitative:
exhaustively over all acts on n-fork branch sets, full resolution to one
actual pays `maxfiber = n` exactly, and partial resolution to `k` images
pays at least `ceil(n/k)`. **Any completion that selects must book exactly
`log2 n`. The no-selector law is not an absence but a refused debt** — the
floor stays debt-free by never collapsing, and lawlike (injective)
evolution is exactly the debt-free sector. The program's two-region thesis
acquires its price column: the forced region is the debt-free class; the
received region is the paid side; and the bill for an actuality is
`log2 n`.
