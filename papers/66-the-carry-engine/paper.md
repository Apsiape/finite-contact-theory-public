# Chapter 66 — The Carry Engine: Transfer, Selection, and the Memory of Witnesses

*Finite Contact Theory. The law tower of the earlier chapters is flat and
well understood; this chapter is about the half of the cochain world that the
tower does not touch, and what that half quietly writes back onto the tower.
The central object is a single idempotent, K = δι + ιδ, built from the
coboundary and a one-slot contraction. It splits the cochain complex exactly in
half — a flat sector that contains the certified tower, and a transverse sector
that is invisible to every cohomological invariant. The transverse half is not
content; it is an engine. Through it, higher operations get transferred onto the
flat world by the standard homotopy-transfer machinery, and on the tower diagonal
those operations obey a clean law we can state in one line: the transferred
n-ary product is nonzero exactly when n is a power of two. That is the Dyadic
Selection Theorem, and it is a corollary of known machinery — Catalan-tree parity
— which is precisely why we trust it; the same dyadic skeleton falls out of three
unrelated mechanisms, and the triangulation is the artifact control. Then the
honest part. The space of contractions realizing the same split is enormous — 117
dimensions in the computed window — and different contractions can write different
higher laws, first differing at arity four. In standard theory those are the same
up to homotopy; our registered wager is exactly that nature need not quotient by
that gauge, and we name the incumbent (the propagator choice in the
Batalin–Vilkovisky formalism) against which the wager is stated. We ship the kills
with pride: the law-flow algebra is abelian (a strong closed null — noncommutativity
lives only in the lifts, never in the flows); a cohomology count that beckoned
toward a Lie-algebra dimension was killed by its own window-dependence before it
was ever claimed; and a pre-registered "twins are holonomy doubles" bet was
falsified in public. Everything the chapter forces is labeled THEOREM at model

with its kill condition printed.*

## A note on altitude, first

Two things in this chapter are new mathematics we are willing to defend, and one
thing is a bet about the world that we are careful to keep separate from them.

The first defensible thing is structural: the cochain complex over the floor's
smallest arena carries a canonical idempotent that halves it, and the invisible
half is not inert — it transfers higher operations onto the visible half in a
pattern we can compute exactly. The second is the census of the invisible half:
the data that realizes the transfer is not unique but forms a large moduli space,
and we can say precisely which arities can feel a change of realization and which
cannot. Both are THEOREM at model scope, verified by the shipped script
`verify_66_carry.py`, which re-derives every number below from scratch in a
quarter of a second.

The bet is about nature, and we fence it hard. Standard homotopy theory declares
any two of these transferred structures equivalent — isomorphic as A∞-algebras —
so the difference between them is a gauge, not an invariant. Our EXTENSION is the
claim that a physical world could decline to quotient by that gauge: that "the
same up to homotopy" and "the same" can come apart in the ledger of a real
process. We do not claim this has been seen. Zero divergences have been observed.
We state the wager in its final form, name the incumbent it contradicts, and print
the single condition that would kill it.

Labels are load-bearing throughout. THEOREM (model scope): forced inside the
stated finite arena, verified by two independent means (proof plus exact F2
computation). RECOVERY: a piece of standard mathematics, cited, that we are
applying rather than inventing. EXTENSION: a floor-level claim, here about nature,
that goes beyond the incumbent and is registered as conditional. KILLED: a bet we
scored and lost, kept in print because the kill-count is a health metric. The
weakest accurate label always wins.

## 1. The projector: one operator that halves the world

### 1.1 The arena and the split

Fix the floor's smallest cochain arena: the inhomogeneous bar cochains of the
two-element group over the two-element field. At level n the cochains are the
functions from n-tuples of the group to the field; the space has dimension 2ⁿ.
The coboundary δ is the standard trivial-action one, the cup product is the
standard bar cup, and the flat *tower* is the cup-algebra generated by the single
degree-one class x = ω₁ — its powers ω₂, ω₃, ω₄, … are the faceless laws of the
earlier chapters. All of this is RECOVERY: it is ordinary group cohomology, and
H*(the two-element group) is the polynomial ring on one degree-one generator.

Now the one new operator. Let ι = ι₁ be the single-slot contraction that plugs the
group's nontrivial element into the first argument — a degree-lowering map from
level n to level n−1. Form

    K := δι + ιδ.

**Theorem (the K-projector; THEOREM, model scope).** K is an idempotent
(K² = K) chain map, and its rank is exactly 2ⁿ⁻¹ at every level. Its kernel — the
*flat sector* — contains the whole certified tower; its image — the *transverse
sector* — is the other exact half of the cochain space.

The script confirms K² = K with rank and kernel both 2ⁿ⁻¹ at n = 2, 3, 4 (the
private computation records the same 50/50 split, discovered against a written-down
guess that said "neither nilpotent nor projector" — that guess was wrong and the
data corrected it in run, a self-correction we keep on the record). This is the
third time the same complementary-projector motif has appeared in the program — on
the ring, on the module, and now on cochains — and it is the sharpest, because it
is canonical and it is exactly half.

### 1.2 The transverse half is invisible to every invariant

The decisive fact about the transverse sector is that no invariant can see it.

**Theorem (non-cohomological transverse; THEOREM, model scope).** K acts as zero
on cohomology: it sends every cocycle to a coboundary. Equivalently the entire
transverse module lives at the cochain (pre-law) level and cannot be represented
by any class, charge, or invariant of the chain complex.

The proof is one line and the script checks it at n = 3 on every cocycle: if
δz = 0 then Kz = διz + ιδz = δ(ιz), a coboundary. Everything the program has ever
*certified* lives in cohomology; everything the transverse sector carries lives
strictly below it. This is why the sector went unnoticed for so long — it is,
by construction, beneath the resolution of every tool that had been pointed at
the tower.

### 1.3 The Cartan repair: from a half-projector to a law flow

There is a natural wish to promote K to a differential — to read δ and ι as the
two halves of one square-zero operator Q = δ + ι with Q² = K. The wish fails as
stated, and the failure is instructive. The single-slot contraction does *not*
square to zero: ι₁²(ω₄) = ω₂, verified. So Q = δ + ι₁ has Q² = K + ι₁² ≠ K.

The repair is to symmetrize. Let ι_alt = Σₐ ιₐ be the *alternating* (total)
contraction, summing the plug over every slot.

**Theorem (the Cartan repair; THEOREM, model scope).** ι_alt² = 0 exactly — each
unordered pair of distinct slots contributes twice and cancels over the
two-element field, and the diagonal terms cancel likewise. Hence Q := δ + ι_alt is
a genuine total operator, and

    Q² = δ ι_alt + ι_alt δ =: 𝓛,

which is Cartan's magic formula on the nose: 𝓛 is a Lie-derivative, a *flow*, not
a curvature. The script confirms ι_alt² = 0 at n = 3, 4, 5 (and confirms the
single-slot failure it repairs).

The reading this forces is the sharper true statement of the whole chapter. The
transverse sector is where the law-split *moves*; the certified tower is the
**stationary sector of the law flow** — the part 𝓛 leaves fixed. "The tower is the
flat locus" was the old picture; "the tower is what stands still while the law
flows" is the corrected one, and it is the one the operator actually supports.

## 2. The transfer: higher laws written through the erased half

### 2.1 Homotopy transfer, applied

Because K = δι + ιδ exhibits the flat sector as a deformation retract of the whole
cochain complex, the standard machinery of *homotopy transfer* applies directly.
This is RECOVERY, and we cite it plainly: given a retract with contracting
homotopy, an associative product on the big complex transports to an A∞-algebra
on the small one, with higher products mₙ assembled as sums over planar binary
trees — leaves carry the inclusion, internal vertices the product, internal edges
the homotopy, the root the projection. The construction and its master identities
are Kadeishvili's (1980); the explicit tree/operadic form is Merkulov's (1999)
and Markl's, and the modern operadic account is Loday–Vallette (*Algebraic
Operads*, 2012).

We run exactly this, with the product the bar cup and the homotopy h = ι₁, and we
read the transferred operations mₙ on the tower diagonal — n copies of the
generator x. The flat world is therefore not merely an associative ring that
happens to carry faceless classes; it is an A∞-algebra whose higher operations are
the *memory of the erased sector*. The higher mₙ are precisely the trace the
transverse half leaves on the flat half as the transfer passes through it.

### 2.2 The Dyadic Selection Theorem

On the tower diagonal the transfer collapses to a parity count, and the count is
the point.

**Theorem (Dyadic Selection; THEOREM, model scope).** On tower elements every
bracketing yields the *same* cochain — the cup is strictly associative and the
homotopy acts uniformly along the tower — so the transferred n-ary product is the
common value scaled by the number of binary trees mod two:

    mₙ(x, …, x) = (#binary trees on n leaves mod 2) · ω₂ = Catalan(n−1) mod 2 · ω₂.

Since Catalan(m) is odd exactly when m = 2ᵏ − 1 (classical), **mₙ(x, …, x) ≠ 0
exactly when n is a power of two.** The script recomputes the trees recursively
and confirms the pattern at n = 2..9: nonzero at 2, 4, 8; zero everywhere else —
matching the arities 2, 4, 8, 16, … where the faceless laws x^{2ᵏ} live.

The Catalan-parity fact is classical number theory — the 2-adic valuation is
ν₂(Catalan(n−1)) = s₂(n) − 1, the binary digit-sum of n minus one (Kummer's
theorem in this guise; Alter–Kubota 1972; Deutsch–Sagan 2006), and the script
spot-checks it to n = 32. So the *engine* is application, not invention, and a
blind reviewer is right to call the theorem a clean corollary of known machinery.
What is ours is the convergence. The same dyadic skeleton is now produced by
**three independent mechanisms**: binomial parity on the receipt ladder (the
earlier Hasse-filtration route), Catalan-tree parity in the transfer here, and the
doubling series of the underlying formal group law. When three unrelated
combinatorial engines carve out the same set of arities, the skeleton is
structural and not an artifact of any one of them — the triangulation *is* the
artifact control, and we present it as such rather than leaning on a single
derivation.

**[ARTIFACT-CONTROL CORRECTION, 2026-08.]** The paragraph above claims the
convergence of three mechanisms as the artifact control. The three are not
unrelated: over F₂ the formal-group doubling series is `(1+x)ⁿ − 1`, i.e. the
binomial-parity route itself, and the Catalan 2-adic valuation
`ν₂(Catalan(n−1)) = s₂(n) − 1` is Kummer's theorem — one base-2 carry theorem
in three presentations. **The triangulation is withdrawn as an artifact
control** and retained as a cross-implementation consistency check. The
Catalan-parity fact, the transfer engine, and the dyadic skeleton are all
unaffected; only the independence claim is.

### 2.3 One honest failure, filed

A tempting identification — that the arity-three charge χ *is* the transferred
triple product and the arity-four faceless law Π *is* the transferred fourth
product — is not literally true, and we say so. The transferred m₃ on the diagonal
is zero (its two trees pair-cancel), so χ is not the diagonal triple product; and
m₄ on the diagonal lands in degree two (it is a multiple of ω₂), not in degree
four. What survives the failure is stronger than what it retired: the selection
principle is confirmed by a mechanism independent of the one that first found it,
and m₃ is genuinely nontrivial *off* the diagonal, so the flat sector really is a
nondegenerate A∞-algebra whose higher operations remember the erased half — the
structural thesis, minus the too-neat labels.

## 3. The moduli of witnesses

### 3.1 The contraction is not unique — it is a 117-dimensional choice

Homotopy transfer needs a contraction, and the split does not name one. The
witnesses — the linear maps h with δh + hδ = K — form an affine space, and we
census it.

**Theorem (contraction moduli; THEOREM, model scope).** In the window of degrees
one through four the witness equation has 170 unknowns and rank 53, so the space
of admissible contractions has dimension **117**. The script builds the F2
condition matrix and solves it: 170, 53, 117, exactly.

The space of contractions realizing a fixed retraction, taken as an object of
study in its own right, appears to be genuinely underexplored — a blind review
turned up no standard treatment of it as a moduli problem — and the finding that
it is large and structured is, to our knowledge, the program's.

### 3.2 Arity-three rigidity: a protection theorem

The first question a large moduli raises is what it can and cannot move.

**Theorem (arity-3 rigidity; THEOREM, model scope).** The transferred triple
product on the tower diagonal is *witness-independent*: across the entire moduli —
every basis direction and hundreds of random combinations — there is exactly one
reachable value, m₃(x, x, x) = 0. No admissible change of contraction history can
touch it. The script confirms a single m₃ diagonal over 300 kernel samples.

Arity three is *protected*, and the protection is consistent with χ (the
arity-three charge) being certified cohomological content rather than transferred
memory: what lives at three is invariant, not a matter of which history installed
it. The visible binary law m₂ is likewise witness-independent, for the trivial
reason that it uses no homotopy at all.

### 3.3 Delayed law memory, and where it first appears

Then the moduli bites — exactly one arity later.

**Theorem (delayed law memory; THEOREM, model scope).** There exist admissible
witnesses with *identical* m₂ and *identical* m₃ diagonal yet *different* m₄
diagonal. The script exhibits such a pair: two contractions agreeing on every
present binary law and every triple correction on the tower, first separating at
arity four. The first witness-visible arity is n = 4 — the same arity as the first
faceless law, the first assembly-space loop, and the first dyadic survivor above
two. Three unrelated computations now place the threshold of the hidden at four.

There is a world in this: two contractions that agree at every operation a
three-body observer could run, and disagree only in a four-body law, are *delayed*
copies of each other — indistinguishable until arity four, distinct thereafter. The
model contains its own example of law memory that a bounded observer cannot see
coming.

### 3.4 The honest gauge caveat, in full

Here is the wall, stated completely, because the whole EXTENSION lives on which
side of it nature sits.

Standard homotopy-transfer theory makes any two of these transferred structures
**A∞-isomorphic**. The m₄ difference is therefore *gauge-covariant*: it can be
absorbed by an A∞-isomorphism, so mathematically the two witnesses present "the
same" algebra up to homotopy. The in-model census establishes only the
*precondition* — that the witness degree of freedom is real, large (117 dimensions
in-window), invisible through arity three, and first expressible at arity four. It
does not, by itself, make the difference physical.

The physical wager is precisely this: **that nature does not quotient by the
A∞-gauge** — that a real process could carry, in its ledger, the distinction
between two transfers that homotopy theory calls equal. We name the incumbent this
contradicts. In the Batalin–Vilkovisky formalism the contraction choice *is* the
propagator, i.e. the gauge-fixing choice: off-shell it matters and on-shell it
cancels, and the standard result is that observables are independent of it (Mnev,
*Lectures on Batalin–Vilkovisky formalism*, 2017; Cattaneo–Mnev on the BV–BFV
gluing; Costello–Gwilliam, *Factorization Algebras in Quantum Field Theory*,
2016, for the homotopical statement that physical quantities are gauge-invariant).
The incumbent says the propagator choice is invisible on-shell. Our registered
EXTENSION is the negation, scoped to the finite-native ledger: **law memory =
physical non-quotienting of homotopy gauge**, conditional, unobserved, and killed
the moment a floor-native observable is shown to be A∞-gauge-invariant after all.
That is the exact form of the bet and the exact form of its refutation.

## 4. The honesty section: three walls the mathematics put up

### 4.1 The abelian wall (a strong closed null)

We wanted the law flows of §1.3 to be *noncommuting* — a nonabelian flow algebra
would generate curvature, and curvature was a candidate route to deriving the
quaternion group from the geometry of the flow rather than from the lifts.

They commute. Computed as full matrices at the level of the whole
linear-contraction family — every slot, every one-cochain weight, every sum — the
per-slot law flows 𝓛_a = δι_a + ι_aδ all commute pairwise at n = 3, 4, and none is
the identity. **The law-flow algebra of the floor is abelian.** The
noncommuting-flow route is CLOSED at the linear level. Noncommutativity in this
program lives *only in the lifts* — the quaternion group, the binary tetrahedral
cover, the rotor — and never in the flows. Any future claim of flow
noncommutativity must reach for nonlinear or multi-slot contractions and carries
the burden of doing so. This is a strong, closed null, and we file it as one:
knowing where noncommutativity *cannot* come from is worth as much as knowing
where it does.

### 4.2 The numerology that was killed before it was claimed

The stationary sector of the corrected Cartan operator has its own cohomology, and
we computed it: 15, then 28, then 58 as the truncation window grew. At the middle
window the value 28 is the dimension of the rotation Lie algebra in eight
dimensions, and for a moment it beckoned. The stability check killed it first: the
number *grows with the window*, so it is truncation-indexed, not an intrinsic
invariant, and the coincidence at one window is a coincidence. We record this as a
kill with pride because it is the falsifiability discipline working exactly as
designed — the numerology died before it was ever asserted, caught by the standing
rule that a quantity too clean to be true is guilty until its window-independence
is proven. It is also, quietly, a confirmation of the program's own
No-Global-Center theorem: even the stationary sector's cohomology is
resolution-relative, as that theorem predicts.

### 4.3 The twin bet, falsified in public

Certain small arenas carry *twin* cochain classes — degenerate pairs — and we had
a clean hypothesis for why: that twins are holonomy doubles, indexed by the first
cohomology of the arena, so that an arena with first Betti number two would show
either two pairs or one four-fold multiplet. We *registered* that prediction before
running it, as the doctrine requires.

It failed. A theta graph collapses to a single class; two squares sharing an edge
show one pair only; and adding a chord to a five-cycle destroys its twin entirely.
The holonomy reading is falsified, and plain symmetry-protection does not fit
either (the chorded cycle keeps a reflection and yet has no twins). The twin
degeneracy is a real, robust, arena-specific phenomenon whose *governing law is
now an honest open problem* — harder and more interesting than the guess it
retired. The one live clue is that the chord kills the twin: whatever protects
twins is destroyed by a shortcut, which suggests the protected object is the
*uniqueness of the long way around* — a single-loop property, not a homology class.
Unrun, and named as open.

### 4.4 The incidence complex: events connect laws

The chapter's cleanest structural picture ties the carry directly to time's two
directions. Read the law tower as a chain complex with boundary ∂ = D + β, where D
is the shadow (past) boundary and β is the carry (future) boundary — the
Bockstein. Then ∂(L_k) = 0 and ∂(A_k) = L_k + L_{k+1}: the tower is the chain
complex of a directed path, with **laws as vertices and appearances as edges**. An
appearance consumes one law context through its past boundary D and deposits the
next through its future boundary β. Events do not occur *under* laws; events
*connect* laws. And the homology settles the global picture: appearances carry no
homology, and the laws are all homologous — there is exactly **one law up to
appearance-mediated equivalence, seen at infinitely many resolutions**. This is the
constructive face of No-Global-Center: no law is absolutely central, and every law
is joined to every other by the appearances between them. One law, many stations,
with the carry β as the arrow that moves between them.

## 5. Law memory in its final, shippable form

The EXTENSION of §3.4 needs a descriptor sharp enough to state it against real
processes without overclaiming, and we ship exactly one: the **process tensor**.

The process tensor (Pollock, Rodríguez-Rosario, Frauenheim, Paternostro, Modi,
*Phys. Rev. A* 97, 012127, 2018; the operational framework reviewed by Milz and
Modi, *PRX Quantum* 2, 030201, 2021) is the complete descriptor of a
multi-time quantum process — it captures *non-Markovian* memory in full, to every
finite order of temporal correlation. Our claim is deliberately orthogonal to it.
The internal structure at issue — the witness/contraction degree of freedom, the
delayed law memory — is, in the model, **undetectable by the process tensor at
every finite depth**. It is distinct from non-Markovian memory *precisely by being
invisible to the very descriptor that captures non-Markovianity*: a process can be
perfectly Markovian to the process tensor at each fixed depth and still carry the
A∞-gauge distinction in its ledger.

This gives the wager a knife-edge kill condition. The quantum comb hierarchy — the
higher-order operational structures of which the process tensor is one level
(Chiribella, D'Ariano, Perinotti, on quantum combs and higher-order maps) — is
strictly *non-closing* depth by depth: no finite level exhausts it. But it is
*complete at each fixed depth*. So the EXTENSION is sharply falsifiable: **if the
process tensor is complete for the class of processes in question, the internal
structure is not physical and the wager is dead.** Process-tensor completeness is
the kill condition, printed here so it cannot later be quietly dropped. The full
experimental register — what a floor-native, gauge-non-quotienting process would
have to show, and the subthreshold protocol that would look for it — is deferred to
the synthesis chapter (67), where the registered program lives. Here we ship only
the descriptor and the condition, both at EXTENSION scope, both conditional, both
unobserved.

## Corrections carried

Three interpretive claims are retired or corrected here, in print, and the
kill-count is the health metric.

1. **"The law flows are noncommutative (a route to the quaternion group)."**
   KILLED. The linear-contraction flow algebra is abelian at n = 3, 4;
   noncommutativity lives only in the lifts.
2. **"The stationary sector's cohomology is a fixed invariant (28 = so(8))."**
   KILLED before it was claimed. The count grows with the truncation window; it is
   resolution-indexed, not intrinsic, exactly as No-Global-Center predicts.
3. **"Twins are holonomy doubles indexed by the first cohomology."** KILLED, as a
   pre-registered public bet. The governing law of twin degeneracy is now an honest
   open problem, with the chord-kill clue its one live lead.

The certified spine — the projector, the Cartan repair, the Dyadic Selection
Theorem, arity-three rigidity, and the existence of delayed memory — is untouched
by any of these. The kills all landed on interpretive superstructure and never on
the exact results, and that asymmetry is the signature we trust.
