# Chapter 39 — The Mortal Observer

*Finite Contact Theory, v0.26. The permission map's one remaining OPEN cell
— body-reading persistence on the mortal floor — is built and measured,
and it closes against the authors' frozen bet: the evasion that Chapter 34
proved absent on the conserving floor exists on the mortal one. The map is
complete: thirteen rows, two floors (three after Chapter 40), no open
cells.*

## The instrument

A body S is a vertex set; protocols are external merges (both ends outside
S); channels are keyed by (merge events, interface(E,S)) — the exact
mirror of the conserving floor's body-reading keying from Chapters 31–35.

## 1. The free shape-lock

On the mortal floor an external merge can *never* touch the body's
interior — verified on every move of the census (75/75): outside events
only move the interface. (The interior-invariance itself is elementary
context preservation, as in Chapter 28's rewriting citations; the
*contrast* is the content.) The conserving observer pays admissibility for
its shape — the body constrains which assignments are lawful. The mortal
observer gets shape for free **and pays in lifespan instead**: shape-lock
costs nothing because the world around the body is being consumed.

## 2. The blindness cell — the evasion, found

Chapter 34's operational blindness law: on the conserving floor, every
bright body-reading channel dies to a single bit reveal — the hunt for a
persistent bright channel (the "evasion") returned empty, exhaustively.
The frozen bet here was that body-reading blinds the mortal floor too —
that blindness is instrument-level. **The bet died: of 32 revealable
bright mortal body-reading channels, 28 are fragile but 4 persist.** The
evasion exists — one floor over. Blindness is genuinely floor ×
instrument: body-reading *blinds* the conserving floor and only *dims* the
mortal one, whose cardinality-flavored correlation survives even the
blinding instrument. This closes the map's last open cell and completes
the third-axis story of Chapter 37.

The right classical anchor, engaged preemptively: the
Wigner-Araki-Yanase theorem, in which conservation laws obstruct ideal
nondisturbing measurement. Our blindness table is WAY-shaped —
conservation is the floor on which clean readout of bright correlation
fails — but the obstruction here is combinatorial (parity supports
splitting into sub-pairs) rather than commutator-based, and the mortal and
breathing floors' partial escape is the table's content. The
einselection/pointer-state lineage is adjacent.

## 3. The finite biography

Every mortal observer reads a terminating text: the world runs out of
outside (max reading depth 3 on the C6 body, against the population bound
of 4). The interface spectrum is coherence-poor but never precluded — 61
bright, 509 partial, **zero dark** — the positive floor of Chapter 36
persists in reading keying. The mortal refusal-table analog is printed in
the shipped output for the cross-floor discriminator record.

## 4. One honest inconclusive

The grammar-under-the-instrument question — does interface keying filter
the mortal floor's cardinality grammar down to parity? — came back
undiscriminating: all 86 faithful supports at this scope are consistent
with *both* grammars (they are small enough to be simultaneously affine
and weight-union). Named open, with the fix identified: larger bodies
break the degeneracy at three or more undetermined bits.
