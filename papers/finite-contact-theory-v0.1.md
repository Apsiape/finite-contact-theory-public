## Abstract

Finite Contact Theory is a finite reconstruction program for admissible
reality. It starts below spacetime, probability, observers, Hilbert space, and
the completed continuum. Its primitive posture is finite contact under
one-use composition: differences compose, each composition consumes its
occasion, and public content is whatever survives admissible reception,
quotienting, and gluing.

Finite Contact Theory is a long-range program to rebuild the physical stack from
finite contact. This v0.1 release publishes one chapter of that program — the
quantum-boundary result at binary-Bell scope — and holds everything beyond that
chapter open by name. Section 8 situates the release against prior
reconstructions of quantum theory and states precisely what is new and what is
recovered.

This v0.1 release does not claim to derive all physics. It publishes a scoped
theorem stack and a public audit discipline. The stack runs from finite contact
to witness counting, from counting to one-receiver gluing, from gluing to
rational Born weights, from the CHSH/Pell boundary to a native binary-Bell
carrier lift, and from that lift to a scoped quantum-boundary result at finite
binary carrier scope. The release also records what remains open: the general
quantum selector, q >= 3 and more-outcome carriers, cross-site interlocking,
gravity sourcing, observer-lattice registration, and nature-facing prediction
bridges.

## 1. What Is Being Reconstructed

The theory asks what can be reconstructed if familiar primitives are not granted
at the start:

- not a completed world;
- not a background spacetime;
- not a primitive probability measure;
- not an observing mind as selector;
- not a Hilbert host;
- not the completed real continuum.

Instead the release starts with finite staged fragments and asks which
structures are forced, recovered, measured, admitted, held, or withdrawn.

The public repository is therefore not a manifesto and not a private lab dump.
It is a curated release with four controls:

1. a status label for every public claim;
2. a scope fence for every theorem or measurement;
3. an evidence posture for each source;
4. a correction ledger for failed or demoted claims.

## 2. Primitive Posture

The public axiom spine is:

```text
U0  DIFFERENCE      there are differences
U1  COMPOSITION     differences stand in further differences
U2  EXHAUSTION      every composition consumes its occasion
U3  FINITE REACH    every staged difference is reached finitely
U4  MULTIPLICITY    multiplicity is correspondence-structure
U5  MEASURE         admissible serial measures push through the trace
                    quotient to witness counting
```

These axioms are not a claim that the private project has finished every
classical problem. They are the finite-contact grammar for the public release.
The release tests what follows from that grammar and what must remain admitted
as an overlay or left open.

The most important negative rule is:

> A familiar noun does not enter the public theory for free.

If a document says "world", "observer", "probability", "quantum",
"spacetime", "energy", or "continuum", it must say whether the term is
recovered, dissolved, admitted as an overlay, measured at scope, or still open.

## 3. The Admissibility Calculus

The Admissibility Calculus is the public immune system of the theory. A claim is
not admitted because it is beautiful or explanatory. It must provide:

- a generation rule;
- a transformation rule;
- a failure rule;
- a status label;
- a scope;
- an evidence path;
- residuals or kill conditions.

The labels used in this release include:

- `THEOREM`: proved at the stated scope;
- `MODEL-SCOPE`: demonstrated inside a named construction;
- `MEASURED`: verified numerically or exhaustively at stated scope;
- `RECOVERY`: known math or physics reconstructed in this frame;
- `OPEN`: named hinge or obligation;
- `WITHDRAWN`: failed or demoted claim;
- `NOTHING`: a proposed primitive dissolved by the framework.

The strongest public sentence is never allowed to outrun the strongest scoped
evidence row.

## 4. Counting And Reception

The first reconstruction step is measure.

The theory does not assume probability as primitive. At finite staged-fork
scope, the public measure is witness counting after the relevant quotient. The
release carries two levels:

1. The theorem-level statement: coherent finite multiplicity pushes through the
   trace/reception quotient as counting.
2. The typicality residual: a single received run still requires that the
   received presentation is not in a counted deviant minority.

This is not hidden probability by another name. The ordering is different:

```text
finite witness multiplicity
  -> quotient-compatible counting
  -> concentration over finite presentation families
  -> explicit typicality residual
```

## 8. Relation To Prior Reconstructions Of Quantum Theory

A fair reader asks the sharpest question first: reconstructing quantum theory is
a mature field, the CHSH/Tsirelson value `2*sqrt(2)` is a place where many
different principles already agree, and the tensor certificates used above are
standard. So what, precisely, is new here, and what is recovered?

This section answers head-on. It places Finite Contact Theory among three prior
families, states the overlaps as recovery rather than novelty, and isolates the
genuinely new content and its scope.

### 8.1 Three prior families

- **Operational / axiomatic state-space reconstructions.** These start from a
  generalized probabilistic theory — states, effects, and probabilities as
  primitives — and single out quantum theory with a few axioms, often a
  continuity or purification postulate (Hardy 2001; Chiribella, D'Ariano and
  Perinotti 2011; Masanes and Müller 2011; the Euclidean Jordan-algebra line of
  Barnum and Wilce).
- **Correlation principles and device-independent bounds.** These bound the set
  of allowed behaviors directly from a physical principle (Tsirelson 1980;
  Popescu and Rohrlich 1994; the NPA semidefinite hierarchy, Navascués, Pironio
  and Acín 2007; information causality, Pawłowski et al. 2009; macroscopic
  locality, Navascués and Wunderlich 2010; the graph-theoretic exclusivity and
  local-orthogonality line, Cabello, Severini and Winter 2014, Sainz et al.
  2014). A central lesson of this family is that these principles converge not
  on the quantum set but on the strictly larger *almost-quantum* set (Navascués,
  Guryanova, Hoban and Acín 2015): they agree with quantum theory at CHSH and
  diverge from it in multipartite scenarios.
- **Discrete-substrate programs.** These build from a combinatorial or discrete
  substrate rather than a probabilistic framework (causal sets;
  cellular-automaton proposals). Historically they have not reproduced the
  Bell/Born structure at this level of detail.

### 8.2 Where this work sits, and what it shares

Finite Contact Theory belongs to the third family in ambition — it starts below
probability, from finite one-use contact — but it reaches the objects of the
first two families by *generating* them rather than *positing* them. Two
overlaps must be stated as recovery, not novelty:

- **One-receiver gluing is the exclusivity / almost-quantum lineage.** The
  one-receiver consistency condition used above is a consistent-exclusivity
  condition in the sense of the graph-theoretic program. On its own it reaches
  the almost-quantum body, and at CHSH-family scope it reaches `2*sqrt(2)`
  exactly, as information causality, macroscopic locality, and local
  orthogonality all do. **CHSH therefore does not separate this work from that
  prior literature; here it coincides with it, and says so.**
- **The tensor certificates and the Jordan normal form are recovered.** The
  finite two-involution decomposition (T-08a), the projective binary tensor lift
  (T-08b), and the Sliwa-23/41 tensor caps (T-08c, T-08d) are standard finite
  algebra and standard certificate material. This release imports and recovers
  them; it does not re-derive them.

### 8.3 What is new

Four items, each carrying its scope:

1. **Measure below probability.** The first two families take probabilistic
   behaviors as primitive. This release does not: the Born weights are derived as
   witness counting (T-03, T-04), in the order multiplicity -> counting ->
   concentration -> a named typicality residual. This is closest in spirit to the
   decision-theoretic and envariance accounts of the Born rule (Deutsch 1999;
   Wallace 2012; Zurek 2005), but the licence for counting here is combinatorial
   — the cardinality of a witness fibre — not a decision axiom or a symmetry of a
   given state.
2. **A generated carrier, not an axiomatized one.** The first family
   *characterizes* the quantum state space; this work *grows* the carrier grammar
   — involutions, tensor product, positive state, block form — from one-use
   contact at binary-Bell scope (T-06). The Jordan structure that the
   Barnum–Wilce line takes as an axiomatic endpoint appears here as an output of
   counting.
3. **A constructive selector candidate.** The open problem shared across the
   second family is that no single accepted principle singles out quantum from
   almost-quantum in every scenario. The candidate offered here is not another
   information principle but a finiteness/constructibility condition —
   tensor-carrier stageability — which at the Sliwa-23/41 separators cuts the
   one-receiver (almost-quantum) body down to the tensor-stageable one. This is a
   frontier target (F-01), not a banked theorem, and its guardrail is explicit:
   if each inequality needs a bespoke carrier grammar, the condition is a
   vocabulary wrapper, not a selector.
4. **A combinatorial origin for a continuous parameter.** At tested scope the
   complementarity angle is a witness-overlap fraction, `t(k,n)=k/n` (T-07) — a
   discrete origin for what is normally a continuous Hilbert-space angle.

### 8.4 The honest separation point

Because CHSH cannot distinguish these programs, any claim that this work adds
content beyond the exclusivity / almost-quantum lineage has to be tested where
they differ: the Śliwa tripartite inequalities (Śliwa 2003). At Sliwa-23 and
Sliwa-41 the one-receiver body sits strictly above the finite tensor cap (T-08c,
T-08d), and it is exactly there that tensor-carrier stageability does visible
work. That separation, not the CHSH value, is the honest locus of the
contribution.

### 8.5 What is not claimed relative to prior work

- This work does not out-reconstruct quantum theory in general scenarios; the
  general selector is open (F-01).
- It does not resolve the almost-quantum versus quantum question in general; it
  offers a candidate mechanism at scope.
- It does not claim that all quantum mechanics has been derived.
- It advances no nature-facing prediction that would distinguish it empirically
  from standard quantum theory.

The contribution is therefore precise: a finite, constructive substrate that
*generates* the measure, the carrier, and — at binary-Bell scope — the boundary
that other programs take as primitive or characterize axiomatically, together
with a scoped, constructive candidate for the selector the correlation-principle
family leaves open.

## 9. Continuum As Admitted Completion

The completed continuum is not a primitive in this release. Finite predictive
content must be reconstructed horizon by horizon, and completed real-continuum
objects are admitted overlays unless a finite reconstruction supplies the
needed content.

This is not anti-mathematics. It is a placement rule. Algebraic, rational,
computable, and finite certificate content can be public and load-bearing. The
completed continuum remains a powerful overlay whose predictive use must be
accounted for.

The hard residual is not hidden: the interacting-QFT continuum limit is a
universal hard edge, not a problem solved here.

## 10. Aperture, Temperature, Energy, And Gravity-Facing Work

The release distinguishes floor-level contact from aperture-level physics.

Temperature and level-2 thermodynamic energy appear at visible-sector scope
where visible entropy production vanishes. The private research corpus adds a measured bridge:
at tested binary-fork recurrent-chain scope, positive carrier readout and
visible temperature occur on the same detailed-balance / zero-visible-EP sector.

This is not a derivation of Einstein gravity. Gravity-facing work remains open:

- sourcing is not settled;
- horizon/state-side export mechanisms were demoted at tested scope;
- current-side or aperture-side accounts remain frontier work.

The public claim is that the theory has a disciplined address for the problem,
not that the problem is solved.

## 11. What Was Killed Or Demoted

The correction ledger is part of the result. Several attractive claims failed:

- binary arity as uniquely forced by matter-memory coincidence;
- matter fixed at dimension 2 across arities;
- closure dynamics selecting Tsirelson;
- a simple arrow statistic as nature-facing divergence;
- graph-floor gravity as generic GR divergence;
- raw capacity as memory;
- completed continuum as native object.

These failures are not embarrassments to hide. They are how the public release
keeps the theory from drifting into metaphor.

## 12. What Remains Open

The v0.1 open hinges are explicit:

- general quantum selector beyond binary-Bell finite carrier scope;
- q >= 3 and more-outcome native carrier lift;
- cross-site interlocking and CHSH-weight/magic bridge;

- continuum limit of interacting QFT;

These are not DOI blockers. They are the named next frontier.
