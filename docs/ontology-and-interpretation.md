# Ontology and Interpretation

Status: interpretive companion to the theorem stack (public, plain-language).

This document states the ontology of Finite Contact Theory in the dialect a
philosopher of physics reads. The mathematics lives in the papers and the
theorem bank. What follows is the picture those theorems are a picture *of*.
Every claim below is labeled. A claim is a **theorem** (proved and machine
checked at a stated scope), an **interpretive frame** (how we read the
theorems — a choice, not a result), or **open** (undecided, and named as such).
Nothing labeled interpretive frame should be read as proved. Nothing labeled
open should be read as settled.

## 1. The Actuality Thesis

Start below the usual furniture. The theory does not begin with a world, a
spacetime, a Hilbert space, a probability, or an observer. It begins with
finite differences that compose, that are consumed when they compose, and that
are reached in finitely many steps. These are the primitives. The one that does
the interpretive work is consumption: a compositional event — an **occasion** —
is used once and not reused.

From that floor the theory reads reality as split in two. This split is the
**interpretive frame** of the whole program.

On one side is the **forced**. These are the features the rules require: the
shape of the fork that a configuration stages, the grammar by which occasions
compose, the constraints any completion must satisfy. The forced is what you can
derive. Much of it is recovery of known structure, and the theory says so.

On the other side is the **received**. This is which completion actually occurs
— which branch of a staged fork is the one that happens. The received is never
derived. The theory's central law is that the floor stages a fork and forces no
selector. The refusal to derive which-outcome is not a gap we hope to close
later. It is the load-bearing claim. If a floor-native selector existed, the
theory would be false as stated.

Two results give this frame its spine.

First, the receiver is a **gluing condition, not a subject** (interpretive frame,
resting on the reconstruction theorems). When local fragments of contact are
made jointly receivable, the condition that lets them close into one coherent
whole is what we name the receiver. It selects nothing. It has no beliefs, no
viewpoint, no interior. We never personify it as a mind. It is the co-reception
constraint and no more. Where a single-receiver gluing condition is imposed,
known structure follows — rational Born-rule weights as counting through the
gluing, and, at a retained interface, a quaternionic state-and-receiver cell
whose closure is the 24-cell and the F₄ root system (**theorems**, at the stated
finite scopes).

Second, actuality has an **exact price** (**theorem**). Resolving an n-way fork
down to one actual outcome books exactly log₂ n fresh one-use marks. The same
number is three things at once: the registers needed to make the collapse
reversible, the record length needed to be certain of the outcome, and the depth
of yes/no questions needed to identify it. Collapsing the fork, knowing the
result, and remembering it are one ledger entry. From this the no-selector law
reads as a refused debt: the floor stays debt-free precisely by never
collapsing, and lawlike (reversible) evolution is exactly the debt-free sector.
The forced region is the debt-free class. The received region is the paid side.
An actuality costs log₂ n. This is finite counting — no probability measure, no
thermodynamic postulate — and its component facts are classical and cited as
such.

So the Actuality Thesis, stated plainly: **the law is the price and the actual
is the payment.** The rules fix what any resolution must cost and must satisfy.
They do not, and by the central law cannot, fix which resolution is paid. That
is received.

**An honest boundary, stated up front.** Whether occasions are the world's
furniture — the actual basic entities — or whether they are bookkeeping over
something deeper we have not reached, is **open**. We do not know. The theory is
consistent with either reading, and the mathematics does not choose. We flag one
temptation here by name and refuse it. An earlier version of the program argued
that the present moment is *real* because it is the most expensive thing to
identify — that access cost proved an ontology of the now. That identification
was **withdrawn** and stays withdrawn. Cost asymmetry supports law-side
invariants against identity expense; it does not prove that the present is
ontologically privileged. We say this in print because respecting the withdrawal
is part of the method: the strong metaphysical claim is gone, and this document
does not quietly reinstate it.

## 2. Positioning Against the Incumbents

The theory owes its lineage honestly. Here is where it stands next to six
research traditions, each named and treated with respect. Kinships are real.
Differences are stated without hedging.

**Relational quantum mechanics (Rovelli).** Kinship: facts are relative, not
absolute. There is no view from nowhere in which an outcome simply *is*; a
resolution is a resolution relative to something. We share that instinct
completely. Difference: in relational quantum mechanics the relatum is an
observer or physical system, and facts are relative to it. Here the relatum is a
gluing condition, not an observer, and it is de-personalized on purpose.
And here the relativized fact carries an exact price — log₂ n fresh marks — which
relational quantum mechanics does not book. Our facts are relational *and*
costed.

**QBism (Fuchs, Schack).** Kinship: an outcome is an act, something that
happens, not a pre-existing value read off a state. We agree that outcomes are
events, not passive facts. Difference: in QBism the quantum state is an agent's
personal degree of belief, and the whole structure is first-personal. Nothing
here is a belief and nothing here is an agent. The receipts are **world-side**.
They are structural conditions on how contact closes, not anyone's expectation
about it. Where QBism locates the probability in a subject, we locate the
resolution in the world and refuse the subject entirely.

**Everett / many-worlds.** Difference first, because it is sharp. Everett keeps
the unitary evolution and denies that any single outcome is selected; all
branches are equally real. This theory does the opposite on exactly that point.
It **prices** the resolution of a fork and treats the received selection as
real: one branch is paid for, the others are not. We do not claim collapse as a
physical process, and we do not claim a mechanism that dynamically kills the
other branches. We make no such dynamical claim at all. What we claim is that
selection is a real, received, exactly-priced input that the floor stages and
does not derive. Everett buys universality by denying selection; we keep
selection and deny that the floor forces it.

**Informational reconstructions (CBH; Hardy; Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti;
and the correlation-principle line).** Kinship: the flavor of the governing laws
is informational and no-broadcast-like. Our forced grammar is no-signaling and
monoidal, and a single-receiver gluing condition reaches the almost-quantum body
and the Tsirelson value exactly where information causality, macroscopic
locality, and local orthogonality reach it. At that boundary we coincide with
this literature and say so; it is recovery, not novelty. Difference: the
**direction of derivation** and the accounting frame. These programs axiomatize
a probabilistic theory — states, effects, probabilities as primitives — and
single quantum theory out with a few postulates. We start below probability,
from finite one-use contact, and *grow* those objects; the license for counting
is the cardinality of a witness fiber, not a probabilistic axiom. Same boundary,
opposite direction, and a ledger the others do not carry.

**Causal sets.** Kinship: a discrete, order-theoretic substrate underneath the
continuum, and a taste for reconstructing physics from combinatorics rather than
positing it. We are in that family in ambition. Difference: our occasions are
**consumed** — one-use — and they leave **receipts**. A causal set is a fixed
partial order of events; our floor is a one-use economy in which composition
spends its occasion and books a record. The order-theoretic substrate is shared;
the consumption discipline and the ledger are ours.

**Process philosophy (Whitehead).** This kinship a review panel found we had
left unclaimed, and it is the closest of all. A consumed one-use occasion, with
a reception that is real but non-subjective, **is actual-occasion-shaped**.
Whitehead's actual occasions are the basic units of process: they happen, they
are once, they perish into the settled past and become data for what comes next.
Our occasions are consumed on use and their receipts become content for later
contact. The resemblance is not decorative; it is structural, and we name it.
Differences, stated cleanly. Ours is **exact finite mathematics, machine
checked** — the price is a theorem, the gluing is a construction, not a
metaphysical narrative. Ours has **no subjective pole**: Whitehead gives every
occasion an interior "feeling," a prehending subject; we give reception no
interior at all, and the de-personalization is enforced, not optional. And ours
has **no God-function**: there is no principle of concrescence-toward-value, no
divine ordering of eternal objects, guiding which occasion becomes what. Where
Whitehead has a subject and a telos, we have a gluing condition and a bill. The
lineage is real and the theory owns it; the differences are what make it a
physics program rather than a metaphysics.

## 3. What Would Change Our Minds

The theory is built to be moved, and it names the moves.

The **received inputs** are the coordinates that would falsify the frame if any
turned out to be forced. There are five: orientation, chirality, world-phase,
genealogy, and scale (**theorem** that these are the residue on the recovered
branch; **interpretive frame** that they are received rather than forced). Each
is staged by the floor and, we claim, selected by nothing in it. If a
floor-native mechanism were shown to *force* any one of them — to derive
chirality, say, or the world-phase, from the floor alone with no received bit —
the central no-selector law would be wrong, and we would have to withdraw the
Actuality Thesis in that sector. Each coordinate has a probe: a real-versus-
complex test in the Renou family for chirality; a holonomy reading for
orientation; a bracketing experiment, where two routes between identical visible
endpoints leave different retained receipts, for genealogy.

The **actuality door** is the other open front. Given a physical substrate that
realizes the floor's dynamics, three candidate measures over its records compete
for the role of "what happens": plain counting, the linear reading of the coat,
and the squared reading. They are exactly discriminated by the sign of their
coarse-graining defects — counting never violates, the linear reading violates
one-signed, the squared reading violates both signs — so one clean measurement
would decide among them (**theorem** for the discrimination; **open** for which
one nature uses). We name no substrate for it yet, and we say so.

Beyond these, one registered prediction genuinely diverges from quantum
mechanics rather than recovering it: a three-contact sector whose every
passive-linear-optical probability is nonnegative yet lies outside the Hilbert
positive-semidefinite cone, predicting a negative three-state Gram discriminant
where ordinary quantum mechanics forces it nonnegative (**theorem** for the
internal-consistency result; **open**, experiment-gated, for whether nature
realizes the sector). It waits on a dedicated experiment.

The closing statement is the honest one, and it is the bounded claim the
fixed-point work reaches, not a triumphal one. **The floor does not uniquely
derive quantum mechanics.** It derives a constrained carrier-and-recovery
architecture, and standard finite-dimensional complex quantum theory sits on one
named branch of it, reached only after five received inputs are supplied. What
is forced is the price and the grammar. What is received is which world got paid
for. And whether the occasions doing the paying are the furniture of reality or
the bookkeeping of something deeper remains, by our own lights, open.
